# 2026 Atlantic low-income tax reductions + Quebec credit rate

How this was researched: this session's network blocks the official hosts (www.canada.ca, www2.gnb.ca, gov.nl.ca, assembly.nl.ca, novascotia.ca, revenuquebec.ca, cdn-contenu.quebec.ca all returned ENOTFOUND or a proxy 403). None of the official pages, the T4127 or the 428 forms could be opened directly. All figures below come from web-search snippets of the named pages. To verify them, allow those domains and re-read the pages.

## Part A

### Newfoundland and Labrador: Low Income Tax Reduction (LITR)
- 2026 basic (individual) maximum: $842. Source: NLR 18/26, Low Income Tax Reduction Regulations for the 2025 and 2026 Taxation Years, para 3(b), the amount for s. 21.1(2.1)(a). https://www.assembly.nl.ca/Legislation/sr/regulations/rc260018.htm . Official. Confidence: medium-high (search snippet; filed May 14, 2026). The amount drops from $997 because Budget 2026 raised the basic personal amount (BPA) to $15,000.
- 2026 spouse/dependant amount (s. 21.1(2.1)(b)): NOT SOURCED. The snippet cut off before it. Left blank. (2025: $557.)
- 2025 for comparison: $997 / $557 (NLR 18/26 para 3(a)). Official. High.
- Threshold where phase-out starts: 2025 individual $23,928 and family $40,460 (gov.nl.ca LITR page: "eliminate provincial income tax for individuals with net income up to $23,928 or for families with net income up to $40,460. Partial ... up to $30,159 ... families up to $50,173"). https://www.gov.nl.ca/fin/tax-programs-incentives/personal/lowincometaxreduction/ . Official. Confidence: high for 2025.
- 2026 threshold: $24,191 per EY (third party). This equals $23,928 × 1.011, and 1.1% is the CRA 2026 NL indexing factor. Not official. Confidence: medium.
- Phase-out rate: 16%. Stated by EY (non-official). It is consistent with the official 2025 figures: (30,159 − 23,928) × 16% = $997 and (50,173 − 40,460) × 16% = $1,554 = 997 + 557. Confidence: medium-high.
- Fully eliminated (2026, individual): not officially stated. EY's $30,491 used the pre-budget $1,007 amount, so it is outdated. Our own arithmetic with $842 gives 24,191 + 842/0.16 ≈ $29,454. That is a derived figure, not a sourced one.
- Income basis: the reduction uses adjusted net income, with separate individual and family thresholds (family = with spouse). Source: gov.nl.ca page wording.
- Side note: CRA payroll (T4127 July 2026) shows the NL BPA going from $11,188 to $13,094 mid-year for payroll purposes. The annual BPA is $15,000 (Budget 2026). The LITR amount for July payroll was not confirmed.

### New Brunswick: Low-Income Tax Reduction
- 2026 threshold: $22,358 (single filer; adjusted family net income). Sources: EY 2026 NB tax rates (https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-ca/services/tax/tax-calculators/2026/ey-tax-rates-new-brunswick-2026-01-15-v1.pdf), wowa.ca and calculatorscanada.ca. This equals the official 2025 $21,920 × 1.02, and 2.0% is the CRA T4032-NB 2026 indexing factor. Not official for 2026. Confidence: medium-high.
- 2025 threshold: $21,920 at a 3% phase-out rate. Source: GNB Finance personal income tax page, https://www2.gnb.ca/content/gnb/en/departments/finance/taxes/personal.html . Official. High.
- Phase-out rate: 3% (GNB). Official. High.
- Maximum: 2025 basic $802 plus $802 for a spouse or eligible dependant, with a total cap of $1,604 (H&R Block, wowa). Not official. Medium. 2026: wowa gives $818 + $818 = $1,636 (802 × 1.02). EY's elimination point implies about $817. Not official. Medium. Basic 2026 amount: $817–818.
- Fully eliminated: EY gives 2026 $49,592 (single) and 2025 $48,620. Not official.
- Income basis: adjusted FAMILY net income (CRA NB428 guide, lines 70–75 in 2025; one spouse claims). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/new-brunswick/5004-pc.html . Official. High.

### Nova Scotia: Low-Income Tax Reduction
- Basic $300; spouse/common-law partner or eligible dependant $300; $165 per dependent child. Sources: NS Finance "Overview of the NS tax system" (2011, official, https://novascotia.ca/finance/site-finance/media/finance/overview_of_ns_tax_system_2011-04-04.pdf). The CRA 2025 NS guide confirms "Claim $165 for each dependant" (children born 2007 or later). Confidence: medium. No 2026 official confirmation was found, and nothing showed the LITR is indexed (NS indexed the brackets and BPA at 1.6% for 2026, OIC 2025-274).
- Threshold: $15,000 adjusted family net income. Phase-out rate: 5%. Sources: NS Finance 2011 overview (official, old) plus 2026 calculators (wowa/ebsource). Confidence: medium.
- Fully eliminated: depends on family size. Single = 15,000 + 300/0.05 = $21,000 (wowa says "about $21,000").
- Income basis: adjusted FAMILY net income (NS428 lines 65–70 in 2025, per the CRA 2025 NS guide). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/nova-scotia/5003-pc.html . Official. High.

### Prince Edward Island: Low-Income Tax Reduction
- 2026 threshold: $23,000 (from $22,650 in 2025). Sources: PEI 2025 budget measures, described by EY/MNP as "$22,650 and $23,000 for 2025 and 2026". The PEI Operating Budget page confirms the threshold increase effective January 2026, without the figure in the snippet: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/finance-and-affordability/provincial-budget-operating-budget . EY 2026 budget alert: https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0879-canada-prince-edward-island-budget-2026 . Confidence: medium-high.
- Phase-out rate: 5% (EY: "additional 5% ... income between $23,001 and $30,000"). Not official. Medium-high.
- Maximum: $350 self, $350 spouse/common-law partner (or eligible dependant), $300 per dependent child. The child amount comes from older CRA PE428 guidance ("Claim $300 for each dependant"). The $350 is consistent with EY's range (7,000 × 5% = 350). Not confirmed on an official 2026 page. Medium.
- Fully eliminated (single): $30,000 (EY).
- Income basis: adjusted FAMILY net income (CRA 2025 PEI guide: "adjusted family income threshold ... has increased"). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/prince-edward-island/5002-pc.html . Official. High.

## Part B: Quebec
- 2026 first-bracket rate: 14% on taxable income up to $54,345. Brackets: 19% to $108,680, 24% to $132,245, 25.75% above. Official Revenu Québec wording: "The taxable income thresholds have been indexed; however, the income tax rates remain unchanged for 2026." https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/source-deductions-and-employer-contributions/employers-principal-changes-for-2026/ and https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/income-tax-return/completing-your-income-tax-return/income-tax-rates/ . Finance Québec, Parameters 2026, Table 3: "Seuil maximal de la première tranche de revenu imposable 53 255 [2025] 54 345 [2026]". https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/adm/min/finances/publications-adm/parametres/AUTEN_IncomeTax2026.pdf . Official. High.
- Rate used to convert non-refundable credit amounts: 14%. Confirmed in words only by third parties: KPMG 2026 Quebec NRTC table, "Tax rate applied to credits 14%" (https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ca/pdf/2026/01/ca-quebec-non-refundable-tax-credit-rates-and-amounts-for-2026.pdf), and TaxTips (the 2023 Quebec budget cut the lowest rate, which "reduced the rate for personal tax credits to 14%"). No official Revenu Québec or Finance Québec page stating "14%" for the credit conversion could be retrieved; TP-1 line 399, TP-1015.F and the Parameters PDF were all unreachable. Confidence: high that 14% is correct. Official wording NOT obtained.
- 2026 basic personal amount: $18,952 (Finance Québec Parameters 2026, Table 3: "Basic personal amount 18 571 | 18 952"; indexation 2.05%). Official. High. 18,952 × 14% = $2,653.
- Exceptions inside lines 350–398 that use other rates (official Revenu Québec line pages): donations 20% on the first $200, then 24%/25.75%; union/professional dues 10%. A calculator should not apply 14% to everything.
