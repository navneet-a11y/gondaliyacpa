PSB column: business expenses other than salary are treated as denied under paragraph 18(1)(p) of the Income Tax Act. Salary paid to you is still deducted. If some costs are benefits or allowances for you or other allowed items, the real PSB figure would be lower.

Not a PSB column: the small business rate applies to the first $500,000 of taxable income and 26.5% applies to any amount above that.

Assumptions: an Ontario corporation, a 12-month taxation year, the full $500,000 small business limit (no associated corporations), a 9% federal small business rate, an Ontario small business rate of 3.2% before July 1, 2026 and 2.2% from July 1, 2026, and no change in current rates for later years.
