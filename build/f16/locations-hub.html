The T2 is a federal return, so the filing itself does not change with the province. What changes is the registry filing that sits beside it, and each province page states that filing and its deadline.

Nil T2 Filing in Ontario

Ontario corporations file the T2 federally. The Ontario annual return is filed separately through the Ontario Business Registry and is not covered by the fee.

Nil T2 Filing in British Columbia

British Columbia corporate income tax is collected through the federal T2. The BC annual report is filed separately with BC Registries and Online Services.
