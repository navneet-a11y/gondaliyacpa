Nil T2 Filing Tools and Calculators

Free tools and calculators for a nil T2 corporate return in Canada. Deadlines, T2 Short eligibility, GIFI schedules, unfiled years and more. Nil T2 filing is $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

Every Tool on This Site

Tools published: [cnf_child_count type="nil_calculator"]

Each one answers a single question from the figures you enter, on one screen, with no sign-up. None of them is a filed return.

Nil T2 Deadline Calculator

Enter your fiscal year-end and get the T2 due date, the next business day where it falls on a weekend or holiday, and the business days remaining.

T2 Short Eligibility Checker

All eight T2 Short conditions on one screen. The checker names the condition that fails, where one does.

GIFI Schedule Requirement Checker

Five questions decide whether a nil T2 needs GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141. Any one yes makes all three required.

Unfiled Years Cost Calculator

Enter how many tax years are unfiled and get the total, at $300.00 plus HST per tax year, with each year filed as its own return.

Schedule 50 Shareholder Threshold Checker

Enter each shareholding and the checker names every shareholder at 10% or more, who must be listed on Schedule 50.

53-Week First Tax Year Checker

Enter the incorporation date and a proposed year-end to see whether the first tax year is within the 53 weeks it may not exceed.

Schedule 24 First Return Checker

Three questions decide whether your T2 is the corporation’s first return and therefore carries Schedule 24.

Federal Annual Return Anniversary Calculator

Enter your federal incorporation date for the annual return due date, 60 days after the anniversary, at a $12.00 government fee.

Dormant Corporation Test

Ten questions decide whether the corporation is nil for the tax year, or has activity that puts it outside a nil return.

GST/HST Nil Filing Checker

Three questions decide whether an open GST/HST or payroll account still needs nil filings, or whether Form RC145 closes it.
