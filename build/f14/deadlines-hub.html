Nil T2 filing in Canada › T2 filing deadlines
· Last reviewed: October 15, 2026

Every resident corporation, including an inactive one, must file a T2 return within six months of its fiscal year-end, every year, until it is formally dissolved. A final T2 is due within six months of dissolution. Having no revenue, no bank activity and no employees does not remove the obligation.

Where a due date falls on a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday recognised by the Canada Revenue Agency, a return received the next business day is on time.

When a Due Date Falls on a Holiday

Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude Ontario statutory holidays. The T2 deadline calendar lists every due date alongside the Ontario statutory holidays that move one.

Behind on Several Years

An unfiled year stays open, invites a demand to file and an arbitrary assessment, and blocks a clearance certificate and dissolution. Each year is filed on its own. See catch-up nil T2 filing for the years we can still file.
