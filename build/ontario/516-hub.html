Nil T2 filing in Ontario is the annual T2 return a corporation with no activity in the tax year still files with the Canada Revenue Agency. Every Ontario corporation must file it within six months of its fiscal year-end, every year until it is dissolved. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil.

Ontario Filing Note

Ontario corporate income tax is reported on the federal T2 return, so there is no separate Ontario corporate tax return. The Ontario annual return is a different filing: since October 2021 it is filed through the Ontario Business Registry, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.

Does an Ontario corporation file a separate provincial corporate tax return?

No. Ontario corporate income tax is reported on the federal T2 return filed with the Canada Revenue Agency, so a nil T2 covers both.

Is the Ontario annual return covered by the nil T2 fee?

No. Since October 2021 the Ontario annual return is filed through the Ontario Business Registry. It is not covered by the $300.00 fee, and we tell you if you still owe it.

What if my Ontario corporation is not nil?

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil. Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck.
