The 10% Rule, Stated Exactly

Schedule 50 is required for any shareholder holding 10% or more of the corporation. Exactly 10% is inside the threshold, not outside it.

A nil year does not remove the schedule. Schedule 50 reports who owns the corporation, and ownership does not depend on whether the corporation traded.

What Schedule 50 Reports

For each shareholder at or above the threshold, Schedule 50 carries the name, the percentage of common and preferred shares held, and the business number or social insurance number as applicable.

A corporation held entirely by one person reports that one shareholder at 100%. A corporation with ten shareholders at 10% each reports all ten.

Included in the Fee

Schedule 50 is included in the fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year where it is required. There is no per-schedule charge, and adding shareholders does not change the price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Shareholder at Exactly 10% Included?

Yes. The threshold is 10% or more, so a holding of exactly 10% is reported on Schedule 50.

Does a Nil Year Remove Schedule 50?

No. Schedule 50 reports who owns the corporation, not what it earned, so a year with no activity does not remove it.

What If the Corporation Has Only One Shareholder?

That shareholder holds 100%, which is above the threshold, so Schedule 50 is required and reports the single holding.

Are Preferred Shares Counted Separately?

Schedule 50 carries the percentage of common shares and the percentage of preferred shares held, so a shareholder can appear for either or both.

Does Adding Shareholders Increase the Fee?

No. The fee is $300.00 plus HST per tax year however many shareholders the schedule reports. There is no per-schedule and no per-shareholder charge.
