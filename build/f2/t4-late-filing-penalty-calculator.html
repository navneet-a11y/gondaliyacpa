The Slip Count Sets the Rate, Not the Lateness

Subsection 162(7.01) charges a daily amount for filing an information return late, and the amount per day depends on how many slips the return carries. A return with fifty slips or fewer runs at $10.00 a day with a floor of $100.00 and a ceiling of $1,000.00. Each band above it doubles or more.

Two consequences follow. A small payroll cannot be charged more than $1,000.00 however long the return sits unfiled, and a return only one or two days late still meets the floor, so there is no cheap window for being slightly late.

A dormant corporation is almost always in the first band or has no return at all. Where no remuneration was paid in the year there are no slips, so there is no T4 return to be late with, and the question becomes whether the open account still owes a nil remittance. The T4 nil filing requirement checker settles that, and the payroll nil remittance checker covers the remittance side.

The slip count sets the rate and the ceiling, so two corporations the same number of days late can owe very different amounts.

A Payroll Filing Is Not the Corporate Return

The T4 return is due the last day of February for the calendar year before it, which has nothing to do with the six months that governs the T2. The two filings sit on different calendars, go to different parts of the Canada Revenue Agency and carry different penalties.

Nil T2 filing at $300.00 plus applicable sales tax per tax year covers the federal corporate income tax return. A payroll account and its slips are not part of that fee. Where the account is being wound down, the payroll account closure checker and the CRA account closure sequencer set out the order, and the guide to CRA accounts on a dormant corporation covers every account a quiet corporation still carries.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the penalty for filing T4 slips late?

It depends on the slip count. One to fifty slips is $10.00 a day, with a minimum of $100.00 and a maximum of $1,000.00.

When is the T4 return due?

The last day of February for the preceding calendar year.

Is there a minimum penalty?

Yes. Each band has a floor, so a return even one day late reaches at least that amount.

Is there a maximum?

Yes, per band. The first band caps at $1,000.00 however long the return stays unfiled.

What if the corporation paid no wages?

Then there are no slips and no T4 return to file late. Whether the open payroll account still owes a nil remittance is a separate question.

Does this penalty apply to the T2?

No. The T2 has its own late-filing provisions, which are percentages of tax owing rather than a daily amount.

Is the penalty per slip or per return?

Per return. The slip count selects which band the return falls in.

Does a nil T2 fee cover T4 slips?

No. The fee covers the federal corporate income tax return. Payroll filings sit outside it.

Can the penalty be cancelled?

It can be the subject of a taxpayer relief request on specified grounds, within the ten-year window those requests carry.

Do weekends count in the days late?

The charge is a daily one and runs on calendar days once the return is past its due date.
