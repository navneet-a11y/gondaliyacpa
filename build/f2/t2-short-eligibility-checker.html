The Eight Conditions

The T2 Short is available only to a corporation that meets every one of these. One failure puts the corporation on the full T2.

ConditionWhat it means
Canadian-controlled private corporationThroughout the tax year, not merely at the year-end
Nil net income or a lossFor income tax purposes, not for accounting purposes
One permanent establishmentA second province or a second fixed place of business ends it
No taxable dividends paid or receivedEither direction disqualifies
Canadian currencyA functional currency election ends it
No refundable creditsClaiming one puts the corporation on the full return
No Ontario transitional tax debitApplies to certain Ontario corporations
No section 34.2 amountApplies where a corporation has a stub-period accrual

The Fee Is the Same Either Way

Qualifying for the shorter form does not change the price, and failing to qualify does not raise it. Nil T2 filing is $300.00 plus HST per tax year whichever form the corporation files on.

Qualifying also does not remove a schedule that is otherwise required. The schedules are decided by what the corporation holds and who owns it, not by which form carries them.

Where the Corporation Is Not Nil at All

A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil. Those are handled by Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year.

A corporation with taxable dividends received fails the T2 Short test and is also outside a nil return altogether, so the checker returning "not eligible" on that condition is worth reading twice.
