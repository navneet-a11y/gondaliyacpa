The Rule, Stated Exactly

Schedule 24 is filed with the first T2 after incorporation. It reports the corporation’s first-time filer information, and it belongs on that return only.

First means first ever, not first this year. A corporation catching up five unfiled years files Schedule 24 with the oldest of them, because that is the first return.

Catch-Up Years and the First Return

Where several years are filed together, they are transmitted oldest first, and Schedule 24 rides with the oldest. The later years do not carry it.

Each of those years is still a separate return at $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

Included in the Fee

Schedule 24 is included in the fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year where it is required, alongside GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141, Schedule 50 and Schedule 8. There is no per-schedule charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does First Return Mean First This Year?

No. It means the first T2 the corporation has ever filed. A corporation catching up several unfiled years files Schedule 24 with the oldest.

Does Schedule 24 Go on Every Return?

No. It belongs on the first return only. Once an earlier T2 has been assessed, Schedule 24 does not go on later ones.

Is Schedule 24 Needed on a Nil First Return?

Yes. The schedule reports first-time filer information, not income, so a nil first return carries it the same as any other first return.

Does Schedule 24 Cost Extra?

No. It is included in the fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year where it is required, and there is no per-schedule charge.

What If the Corporation Was Incorporated Years Ago?

The first return is still the first return. It is filed for the oldest unfiled tax year, carries Schedule 24, and the later years follow in order.
