Two Numbers, and Neither Moves

A federal corporation files its annual return within 60 days of the incorporation anniversary, and the fee is $12.00. Both are fixed, which makes this the most predictable obligation a corporation has.

It is a government fee and HST does not apply to it. So the total this page gives is the total: there is no tax line to add to it, unlike every other price on the site.

The Date Runs From the Anniversary

Not from the fiscal year-end, which is what makes this obligation drift out of sight. A corporation whose T2 is due in December and whose anniversary falls in March has two filing dates nine months apart, every year, both correct.

Changing the fiscal year-end does not move this date either. The federal annual return anniversary calculator works out a single year’s dates in more detail than the table here does.

It Is Not a Tax Return and Missing It Is a Different Problem

The annual return reports the directors and the registered office to Corporations Canada. It reports no income and no tax, and filing it says nothing to the Canada Revenue Agency.

Missing it does not create an unfiled tax year. It puts the corporation at risk of being struck from the register, which is a different consequence with a different cure. The annual return versus tax return checker sets the two filings side by side.

Outside the Nil T2 Fee, and Small

This filing is not covered by the nil T2 fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year. It is a registry filing, made to the registry, and a corporation can make it itself once it knows the date.

Which is the practical reason this page exists. At $12.00 the fee is not the problem. Not knowing the date is, and a corporation struck off for the want of a $12.00 filing has an expensive problem from a cheap cause.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the federal annual return?

$12.00. It is a government fee and HST does not apply to it.

When is it due?

Within 60 days of the incorporation anniversary.

Does the date move if I change my fiscal year-end?

No. It runs from the incorporation anniversary, so it does not follow the fiscal year-end.

Is the annual return covered by the nil T2 fee?

No. The fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year covers the nil T2 return. The annual return is a separate registry filing.

What happens if I miss it?

It does not create an unfiled tax year. It puts the corporation at risk of being struck from the register.
