The Nil T2 Is One Filing, Not All of Them

A nil T2 reports the corporation’s tax year to the Canada Revenue Agency. It is not the only thing an inactive corporation owes, and the fee covers the T2 alone.

A nil T2 does not cover an Ontario annual return through the Ontario Business Registry, a federal Corporations Canada annual return at $12.00 due within 60 days of the anniversary, an Alberta AT1, a Quebec CO-17, or a nil GST/HST or payroll filing. We tell you which of these you still owe.

Two Different Bodies

Registry filings and tax returns go to different places. The annual return goes to the registry the corporation was incorporated or registered with, federally through Corporations Canada or provincially. The T2 and the programme account returns go to the Canada Revenue Agency.

Missing the registry one is what eventually dissolves a corporation without its owner intending it, which is a separate problem from an unfiled T2.

What the Fee Covers

$300.00 plus HST per tax year covers one nil T2 for one corporation for one tax year, including the T2 Short where the corporation qualifies, the GIFI schedules where they are required, your Form T183CORP package, CPA review, electronic transmission by a registered EFILE electronic filer, and your CRA confirmation emailed within 3 business days.

The $12.00 federal annual return fee is a government fee and tax does not apply to it. It is paid to Corporations Canada, not to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Annual Return the Same as the T2?

No. They go to different bodies and have different deadlines. The annual return keeps the corporation on the registry; the T2 reports its tax year to the Canada Revenue Agency.

Do You File the Ontario Annual Return?

No. It is filed separately through the Ontario Business Registry and is not covered by the fee. We tell you that you owe it.

What About an Alberta AT1 or a Quebec CO-17?

Both are separate provincial returns and neither is included in the fee for the nil T2.

The Corporation Has Been Inactive for Years. Does All of This Apply to Every Year?

The T2 does: one return per tax year until dissolution. Use the unfiled years checker to list the years. The other filings follow their own cycles.

Is It Cheaper to Just Dissolve the Corporation?

Dissolving does not remove the returns already outstanding. The dissolution readiness checker sets out the order the steps run in.
