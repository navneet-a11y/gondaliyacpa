One Price, One Return, One Tax Year

The fee buys the corporate income tax return for one corporation for one tax year: the T2 itself, the GIFI schedules the return requires, Schedule 50 and Schedule 24 where they apply, CPA review, Form T183CORP and electronic transmission by a registered EFILE electronic filer.

What it does not buy is anything filed with a registry or a province, or any account the corporation holds besides its corporate tax account. Those are separate filings on separate dates, and filing the T2 does not satisfy any of them.

A Year That Is Not Nil Is Not Covered Either

This fee is for a tax year with no activity. A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil, and those years are not covered.

Where a year turns out not to be nil we say so before anything further is charged, and the file moves to Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca. Nothing is taken at the higher price without you agreeing to it first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the fee cover my annual return?

No. The Ontario annual return and the Corporations Canada annual return are registry filings, not tax returns, and filing the T2 does not satisfy either.

Does it cover Alberta AT1 or Quebec CO-17?

No. Alberta and Quebec each collect their own corporate tax, so AT1 and CO-17 are separate provincial returns and are not part of the federal T2.

What about GST/HST and payroll?

Both are separate accounts with their own filings. An open account still needs its nil filing even in a year the corporation did nothing.
