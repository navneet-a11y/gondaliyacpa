What Counts as Activity

No revenue of any kind, no expense claimed, no payroll, no asset bought or sold, no dividend paid or received, and no inter-company transaction. Interest credited on a bank balance is income, so a year with interest in it is not a no-activity year, however small the amount.

Share capital, cash, a shareholder loan, an incorporation cost or a bank charge does not stop the year being nil. It does make GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 required, and those are included in the fee.

Where the Corporation Is Not Nil

A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil. Those are handled by Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year.

Dormant Does Not Mean Exempt

A dormant corporation files a T2 within six months of each fiscal year-end, every year, until it is formally dissolved. There is no dormancy form, no status to elect and no threshold below which the obligation stops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bank Interest Make a Year Not Nil?

Yes. Interest credited on a bank balance is income, so a year with interest in it is not a no-activity year, however small the amount.

Is There a Form That Tells CRA a Corporation Is Dormant?

No. There is no dormancy form and no way to pause the T2. The annual return is the only thing that reports the year, and it reports nil amounts where there was no activity.

Does Share Capital Stop a Year Being Nil?

No. Share capital, cash, a shareholder loan, an incorporation cost or a bank charge does not stop a year being nil. It does make GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 required, and those are included in the fee.

Can a Corporation Be Dormant for Many Years?

Yes. There is no limit on consecutive nil years. What causes trouble is a dormant year left unfiled, not the dormancy itself.
