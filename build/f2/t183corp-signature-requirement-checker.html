What Form T183CORP Is For

Form T183CORP is the authorization that lets a return be transmitted electronically on a corporation’s behalf. It carries the figures on your own return, and signing it is how you confirm those figures before anything is sent.

It is always Form T183CORP. The name is never shortened on this site, because the shorter name belongs to a different authorization for a different return and the two are not interchangeable.

An Authorized Signing Officer, Not Just Anyone Involved

The form is signed by an authorized signing officer of the corporation. A bookkeeper, a spouse, a shareholder who is not an officer and the accountant preparing the return are none of them that, however closely they are involved.

The officer also has to have read the figures. The signature is a confirmation of the return’s contents, so signing without reading it is not the thing the form is asking for.

Before Transmission, and the Timestamp Proves It

The form is signed before the return is transmitted, not alongside it and not afterwards. It authorizes the transmission, so it has to exist first.

Here it is signed on a single-use access-controlled screen, and the date and the hour, minute and second are recorded on the server in Eastern Time. It is never backdated. The timestamp is written as you sign, which is what makes the sequence demonstrable rather than asserted.

The Firm Keeps It for Six Years and Does Not Send It

The signed form stays with Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation for six years. It is not sent to the Canada Revenue Agency unless the Canada Revenue Agency asks for it.

That surprises people who expect the signature to travel with the return. It does not. The transmission carries the return; the form is the record of the signature that permitted it, held in case it is ever asked for. The guide to T183CORP signing and transmission sets out the whole sequence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can sign Form T183CORP?

An authorized signing officer of the corporation. A bookkeeper, a non-officer shareholder or the accountant preparing the return cannot sign it in that officer’s place.

When is Form T183CORP signed?

Before the return is transmitted. It authorizes the transmission, so it has to exist first, and the timestamp recorded as you sign shows that it did.

Is Form T183CORP sent to CRA?

No, unless the Canada Revenue Agency asks for it. The transmission carries the return; the signed form is the record of the signature that permitted it.

How long is it kept?

Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation keeps the signed form for six years.

Can the form be backdated?

No. The date and the hour, minute and second are written by the server in Eastern Time as you sign.
