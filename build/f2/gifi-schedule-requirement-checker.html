The Rule, Stated Exactly

A corporation inactive all year with nothing to report may omit GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141, but any share capital, cash, shareholder loan, incorporation cost or bank charge makes them required.

Read the second half carefully. The exemption needs nothing at all to report. A corporation holding $100.00 of share capital issued at incorporation has something to report, so the schedules are required.

Why Each of the Five Counts

TriggerWhy it counts
Share capitalIssued at incorporation and sits on the balance sheet
CashA balance in an account is an asset
A shareholder loanA balance either owed to or by the corporation
An incorporation costCapitalised, so it remains on the balance sheet
A bank chargeAn expense, so there is something on the income statement

Four of the five are present in most corporations from the day they are registered, which is why the exemption is narrower in practice than it reads.

What the Three Schedules Are

Schedule 100 is the balance sheet in GIFI codes. Schedule 125 is the income statement, which on a genuinely nil year reports $0.00 on both sides rather than being left out. Schedule 141 is the notes checklist that records who prepared the financial information.

All three are included in the fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year where they are required. There is no per-schedule charge.
