A Percentage of What Was Understated

Subsection 163(2) of the Income Tax Act charges half of the tax that was understated by a false statement or an omission in a return, with a minimum of $100.00. It is not a late-filing penalty and has nothing to do with when the return arrived.

It also carries an element the late-filing provisions do not: the statement or omission has to have been made knowingly, or in circumstances amounting to gross negligence. Carelessness falling short of that is not enough, and establishing the facts is the Minister’s job rather than the taxpayer’s.

Why This Comes Up on Catch-Up Filings

The provision gets raised in connection with voluntary disclosures, because relief from it is one of the things a disclosure can achieve. That is useful where years carried real income that was never reported. It is beside the point where the years were genuinely nil, which is the more common case for a dormant corporation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the gross negligence penalty?

Subsection 163(2): half of the tax understated by a false statement or omission, with a minimum of $100.00.

Does it apply to filing late?

No. Late filing is subsections 162(1) and 162(2). This one is about what the return said.

Does carelessness count?

Not on its own. The statement must be made knowingly or in circumstances amounting to gross negligence.

Who has to prove it?

The onus of establishing the facts justifying the penalty rests with the Minister.

Can it apply to a nil return?

A return reporting nothing understates nothing, so there is no base for the percentage.

Is it charged instead of the late-filing penalty?

No. It is in addition to any late-filing penalty and any interest.

Is there a maximum?

The provision sets a minimum rather than a maximum; the figure follows the amount understated.

Does a voluntary disclosure remove it?

Relief from this penalty is one of the things the programme can achieve, subject to its conditions.

Does it apply to a corporation or a person?

It applies to the taxpayer who made the return, which for a T2 is the corporation.

What if the understated amount is small?

The $100.00 minimum applies, so a small understatement still reaches that figure once the provision is engaged.
