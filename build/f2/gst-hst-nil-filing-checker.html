An Open Account Files Whether or Not You Trade

An open GST/HST account carries its own filing obligation, and so does an open payroll account. A corporation that stopped trading but left the accounts open still owes nil returns on them for every period.

Filing your T2 does nothing for these accounts. They are separate registrations with separate periods and separate returns.

Closing an Account With Form RC145

Form RC145 closes a GST/HST or payroll account. Closing the account ends the nil filings rather than repeating them every period, which is usually what a corporation that has stopped trading wants.

A corporation that expects to trade again may prefer to keep the account open and file the nil returns in the meantime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Filing a Nil T2 Close a GST/HST Account?

No. They are separate registrations. The T2 is a tax return; the GST/HST account carries its own filing obligation until it is closed.

What Does Form RC145 Do?

It closes a GST/HST or payroll account, which ends the nil filings on it rather than repeating them every period.

Does an Open Payroll Account Need Nil Remittances?

An open payroll account carries its own filing obligation whether or not wages were paid, until the account is closed.

Should a Dormant Corporation Close Its Accounts?

A corporation that does not expect to trade again usually closes them, since that ends the filings. One that expects to trade again often keeps them open and files nil returns in the meantime.

Is Any of This Covered by the $300.00 Fee?

No. The fee of $300.00 plus HST per tax year covers the nil T2 only. We tell you which account filings you still owe.
