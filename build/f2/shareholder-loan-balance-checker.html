A Balance Sitting Still Is Still Something

A corporation inactive all year with nothing to report may omit GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141. A shareholder loan is one of the five things that make them required, and nothing in that turns on whether the balance moved.

So a loan that has sat unchanged for six years is reportable in the seventh. The corporation has something to report, which is the test, and a dormant year does not empty the balance sheet.

Either Direction Is a Shareholder Loan Here

Money the corporation owes its shareholder and money the shareholder owes the corporation are both a shareholder loan balance for this purpose. Bookkeeping distinguishes them and the reporting trigger does not.

Which settles the question people arrive with. Whether the balance is a due-to or a due-from, the answer about the GIFI schedules is the same.

A Loan Does Not Stop a Year Being Nil

A loan balance is a balance sheet item, not income. A dormant corporation carrying one files an ordinary nil return at $300.00 plus HST per tax year, with the GIFI schedules on it.

The thing to separate is the balance from the movement. A balance is reported. Money moving in a year is activity, and activity is where the question of whether the year is nil starts to need a real answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a shareholder loan make the GIFI schedules required?

Yes. A shareholder loan is one of the five things that make GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 required, even where the corporation had no activity.

What if the balance did not change all year?

It still counts. The permission to omit the schedules belongs to a corporation inactive all year with nothing to report, and a balance is something to report.

Does the direction of the loan matter?

Not for this. A balance the corporation owes its shareholder and one the shareholder owes the corporation are both a shareholder loan balance here.

Does a shareholder loan stop the year being nil?

No. It is a balance sheet item rather than income, so a dormant corporation carrying one files an ordinary nil return.
