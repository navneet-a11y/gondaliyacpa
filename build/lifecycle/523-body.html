Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a corporation stay inactive?

There is no time limit, as long as it files a T2 for every tax year and its annual return with the registry. A registry can dissolve a corporation that stops filing annual returns.

Can I file several nil years at once?

Yes. Choose every tax year you have not filed on the form. Each year is a separate nil T2 at $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

What makes a year not nil?

Any activity in the tax year. Investment income, rental income, and dividends paid or received all make a year not nil. If a return turns out not to be nil, you get a full refund.

Who signs Form T183CORP?

An authorized signing officer of the corporation. Sign Form T183CORP on the same screen instantly, as part of the filing form.

When do I get confirmation from CRA?

Within 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Ontario statutory holidays.

Do I need to call or meet anyone?

No call, meeting or email chain needed. The form covers everything. If you prefer to write to us, use info@corpnilfilingfor300.ca.
