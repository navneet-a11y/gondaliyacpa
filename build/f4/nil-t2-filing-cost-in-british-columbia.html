What a Nil T2 Return Costs a British Columbia Corporation

$300.00 plus HST per tax year. Two years is $600.00, three is $900.00 and five is $1,500.00, each before tax, because each tax year is a separate return carrying the same preparation and the same CPA review. There is no reduced rate for filing several years together.

British Columbia is not an HST province, so tax is added at the rate that applies to your province rather than at a single harmonised rate. British Columbia corporate income tax is reported on the federal T2, so there is no second provincial filing fee of the kind an Alberta or Quebec corporation faces.

What the Fee Includes

One nil T2 for one corporation for one tax year, including the T2 Short where the corporation qualifies, GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 where they are required, Schedule 24 on a first return, Schedule 50 for every shareholder at 10 per cent or more, Schedule 8 and loss continuity, your Form T183CORP package with single-use secure e-signing, CPA review, electronic transmission by a registered EFILE electronic filer, one corrected transmission if the Agency rejects the first one, and your CRA confirmation emailed within 3 business days.

The price does not change with the age of the year, the size of the corporation or the number of shareholders.

Who This Is For

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil.

Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck. Filings This Does Not Cover

  • Your British Columbia annual report. It is filed with BC Registries and Online Services within two months after each anniversary of the date of incorporation, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • A federal Corporations Canada annual return, at $12.00, if your corporation is federally incorporated.
  • A nil GST/HST return or a nil payroll return.
  • RC145 closure of a GST/HST or payroll account.
