Nil T2 Filing for Alberta Corporations

A nil T2 is the corporate income tax return an Alberta corporation files for a tax year in which it had no activity. Every Alberta corporation files a T2 with the Canada Revenue Agency within six months of its fiscal year-end, every year until it is formally dissolved, whether or not it earned anything. We prepare the return, a CPA reviews it, and we transmit it electronically for $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

Alberta administers its own corporate income tax, so an Alberta corporation also files the Alberta AT1, a separate provincial income tax return. The Alberta AT1 is not covered by the $300.00 fee.

The T2 due date comes from the Income Tax Act and does not vary by province. The T2 assessment count is refreshed when the Canada Revenue Agency reissues its annual table.

What Makes a Return Nil

A nil return reports no income and no expenses for the tax year. The corporation still exists, still has a Business Number, and still has a fiscal year-end, so the return is still due. Filing it keeps the corporation’s tax record current and keeps its standing with the Canada Revenue Agency intact.

Why Alberta Is Not Like Most Provinces

In most provinces, provincial corporate income tax is collected through the federal T2, so one return settles both. Alberta is one of two provinces that administer their own corporate income tax instead, Quebec being the other. An Alberta corporation therefore has two corporate income tax returns to think about rather than one.

What we file is the federal T2. The Alberta AT1 is a separate return and is not covered by the $300.00 fee, and we tell you that before you pay rather than after.

Who This Is For

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil.

Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck. Filings This Does Not Cover

  • The Alberta AT1, the separate Alberta corporate income tax return. It is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • Your Alberta annual return, filed with the provincial corporate registry. It is a registry filing rather than a tax return, it carries no tax, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • A federal Corporations Canada annual return, at $12.00, if your corporation is federally incorporated.
  • A nil GST/HST return or a nil payroll return.
  • RC145 closure of a GST/HST or payroll account.
