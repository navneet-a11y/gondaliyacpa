Keeping a Dormant Alberta Corporation Up to Date

A dormant Alberta corporation is one that still exists on the register but has stopped trading, or never started. Being dormant does not pause anything. The corporation still files a T2 with the Canada Revenue Agency within six months of each fiscal year-end, every year until it is formally dissolved. If the dormant corporation had no activity in the tax year, we file its nil T2 for $300.00 plus HST. Filing does not dissolve the corporation. It keeps the corporation’s tax record clean while you decide whether to restart it or close it.

Dormant Is Not the Same as Dissolved

An Alberta corporation stays in existence until it is dissolved. Until then every filing obligation continues, and a year of no activity is a year that still has a return due. The corporate tax account and the provincial registry record are separate trails, so bringing one up to date does not bring the other with it.

If you plan to close the corporation instead, see our final T2 before dissolution in Alberta.

A Dormant Alberta Corporation Still Has Two Returns

Alberta administers its own corporate income tax, so an Alberta corporation also files the Alberta AT1, a separate provincial income tax return. The Alberta AT1 is not covered by the $300.00 fee. A dormant corporation does not escape that by being dormant, any more than it escapes the federal T2.

We file the federal nil T2. Where the Alberta AT1 is also outstanding, that is a separate filing, and we say so plainly rather than leaving you to find out later.

Who This Is For

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil.

Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck. Filings This Does Not Cover

  • The Alberta AT1, the separate Alberta corporate income tax return. It is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • Your Alberta annual return, filed with the provincial corporate registry. It is a registry filing rather than a tax return, it carries no tax, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • A federal Corporations Canada annual return, at $12.00, if your corporation is federally incorporated.
  • A nil GST/HST return or a nil payroll return.
  • RC145 closure of a GST/HST or payroll account.
