The Two-Page T2 Short for Alberta Corporations

The T2 Short is a two-page version of the T2 return. An Alberta corporation may use it only where it meets every one of the Canada Revenue Agency’s conditions for the short return. Where it qualifies, we file it, and it is included in the $300.00 plus HST fee rather than charged separately.

We Check Before We File

Meeting most of the conditions is not enough, so we check every one of them against your corporation before we prepare the return. Where the corporation does not qualify, we file the full T2 instead, at the same fee.

Either way the return is reviewed by a CPA and transmitted electronically to the Canada Revenue Agency.

The Short Return Is Federal Only

The T2 Short is a Canada Revenue Agency form, and the conditions for using it are federal conditions. Qualifying for it changes which federal form your corporation files. It does not change what Alberta asks for.

Alberta administers its own corporate income tax, so an Alberta corporation also files the Alberta AT1, a separate provincial income tax return. The Alberta AT1 is not covered by the $300.00 fee. There is no Alberta equivalent of the T2 Short in what we file, because the Alberta AT1 is not ours to file.

Who This Is For

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil.

Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck. We route you to our sister office at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per tax year before you pay. If we find it after you pay, we refund you in full.

How T2 Short Filing in Alberta Works

  1. Type your corporation name. We fill the Business Number and incorporation date.
  2. Pick the tax years to file and confirm the corporation had no activity in each of them.
  3. Complete the attestation and identity check, then pay $300.00 plus HST per tax year.
  4. Sign Form T183CORP. We transmit your return electronically.

Your CRA confirmation is emailed within 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude Alberta statutory holidays.

T2 Short Filing Cost in Alberta

$300.00 plus HST per tax year. Each tax year is a separate return and carries the same fee. Tax is added at the rate for your province. See the full nil T2 filing cost.

Filings This Does Not Cover

  • The Alberta AT1, the separate Alberta corporate income tax return. It is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • Your Alberta annual return, filed with the provincial corporate registry. It is a registry filing rather than a tax return, it carries no tax, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • A federal Corporations Canada annual return, at $12.00, if your corporation is federally incorporated.
  • A nil GST/HST return or a nil payroll return.
  • RC145 closure of a GST/HST or payroll account.

We tell you which of these you still owe.
