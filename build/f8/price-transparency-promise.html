What Sits Outside the Fee, Named

A nil T2 is a tax return, and several things that look like part of it are separate filings to separate bodies:

Two rows on that table are corporate tax and still sit outside the fee. Quebec collects its corporate income tax through CO-17 and Alberta through AT1, and each is a separate return in its own right rather than a schedule of the T2. Everywhere else one T2 settles both.

We tell you which of these you still owe. The fee-covers checker answers it question by question, and the $12.00 Corporations Canada fee is a government fee that HST does not apply to.

If the Year Is Not Nil

This service is for a tax year with no activity. A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil.

Where that is the case we say so before anything further is charged, and the file moves to Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year. No charge is taken at the higher price without you agreeing to it first. If we find it after you have paid, you are refunded in full.
