Why It Is Six Returns and Not One

A T2 corporation income tax return covers one tax year. There is no combined return for several years and no way to report six dormant years on a single form, so six unfiled years means six returns prepared, reviewed and transmitted separately.

Each of the six carries its own GIFI schedules for that year, its own Form T183CORP signed with that year’s figures, and its own confirmation from the Canada Revenue Agency. We are a registered EFILE electronic filer and each year is transmitted electronically in its own transmission. That is the work the fee multiplies against.

The Order They Are Filed In

Oldest year first, then forward in sequence. A corporation carries figures from one year into the next, and even a dormant corporation carries its share capital and its balance sheet forward, so a later year transmitted before an earlier one can be assessed against figures that have not been filed yet.

Filing in order also matters to what the Canada Revenue Agency sees. Six years arriving oldest first reads as a corporation bringing itself current. How to catch up on years of unfiled T2 returns sets out the sequence in full.

What a Nil Year Costs by Sitting Unfiled

A true nil return carries no percentage late-filing penalty, because that penalty is a percentage of tax owing and the corporation owes $0.00. The unfiled year still stays open forever, invites a demand to file and an arbitrary assessment, blocks a clearance certificate and dissolution, and costs a non-resident-owned corporation $25.00 per day up to $2,500.00.

So the cost of six unfiled nil years is not a penalty figure. It is the fee for six returns, plus whatever the open years have already blocked: a clearance certificate, a dissolution, a financing application or a sale that needs the corporation current. If One of the Six Years Is Not Nil

This is the part that only comes up with several years at once. Nil is decided year by year, not for the corporation as a whole, so a corporation can have five dormant years and one year in which it earned interest, paid a dividend or held a rental property.

Where that happens only that year moves. It goes to Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year, and the five nil years stay here at the published fee. One year outside the service does not move the whole file, and nothing at the higher price is charged without you agreeing to it first.

A holding corporation with investment income, an inactive corporation that received a dividend, and a rental corporation are the three cases that catch people out. None of them is nil. The eligibility checker settles it year by year before you pay.

What the Six Returns Do Not Cover

Six years of T2 returns settle six years of corporate income tax with the Canada Revenue Agency. They do not settle six years of annual returns with your registry, which is a separate filing to a separate body and is not a tax return at all. A corporation six years behind on its T2 returns is usually six years behind on those too.
