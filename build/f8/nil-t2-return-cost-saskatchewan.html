Several Outstanding Years, and the Order They Go In

Where a Saskatchewan corporation has several dormant years outstanding, the cost is simple multiplication and the sequence is not. Each year is its own return, and they are filed oldest first.

Why Oldest First

A corporation carries figures from one year into the next. Even a dormant one carries its share capital and its balance sheet forward, so a later year transmitted before an earlier one can be assessed against figures that have not been filed yet.

Six dormant years is six returns at the same fee each. The arithmetic is on the six unfiled years page; the per-year price does not change because there are several.
