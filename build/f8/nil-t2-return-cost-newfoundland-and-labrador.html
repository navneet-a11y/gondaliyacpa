What a Nil T2 Return Costs in Newfoundland and Labrador

A nil T2 return for a Newfoundland and Labrador corporation is $300.00 plus HST per tax year. Tax is added at the rate for your province. Each tax year is its own return and carries the same fee, so six unfiled years are six returns.

The fee does not change by province. What changes between provinces is what else the corporation owes, and that is the useful part of this page.

One Return Settles the Corporate Tax Here

Newfoundland and Labrador levies its corporate income tax through the federal return, so the T2 settles both the federal and the provincial corporate tax for the year. There is no separate provincial corporate return to file.

Two provinces are different. Quebec collects through CO-17 and Alberta through AT1, and each of those is a separate return rather than a schedule of the T2. The provincial return requirement checker settles which, if either, applies to a given corporation.

What Newfoundland and Labrador Wants That the Fee Does Not Cover

The Newfoundland and Labrador registry of companies wants its own annual return. That is a registry filing confirming the directors and the registered office, it reports no income, and filing the T2 does not satisfy it. It is not covered by this fee.

A federally incorporated corporation owes a Corporations Canada annual return instead, at $12.00, due within 60 days of the anniversary. The $12.00 is a government fee and HST does not apply to it. The annual return versus tax return checker separates the two trails, and the three filings compared sets all three side by side.

Several Unfiled Years

Each year is prepared, reviewed and transmitted separately, so the fee multiplies by the number of years. They are filed oldest first, because each year’s closing position carries into the next return. A Year That Is Not Nil

This fee is for a tax year with no activity. A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil.
