What a Nil T2 Return Costs

One tax year is $300.00 plus HST. With Ontario's 13 per cent HST that is $39.00 of tax, so $339.00 in total for the year. The fee is the same for every tax year and every province. Each tax year is a separate return and carries the same fee.

What the Fee Includes

  • Preparation of the T2 corporation income tax return for one tax year.
  • The GIFI schedules that tax year requires.
  • CPA review before the return is transmitted.
  • Form T183CORP generated with that return's figures and signed on a secure single-use screen.
  • Electronic transmission to the Canada Revenue Agency.
  • Your CRA confirmation, emailed within 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude Ontario statutory holidays.
  • One corrected transmission if the Canada Revenue Agency rejects the first one.

What the Fee Does Not Include

  • Your Ontario annual return. Since October 2021 it is filed through the Ontario Business Registry, and it is not covered by the $300.00 fee.
  • A federal Corporations Canada annual return, at $12.00, due within 60 days of your anniversary if your corporation is federally incorporated.
  • An Alberta AT1, which is a separate provincial return.
  • A Quebec CO-17, which is a separate provincial return.
  • A nil GST/HST return or a nil payroll return.
  • RC145 closure of a GST/HST or payroll account.

We tell you which of these you still owe.

Work That Is Not Nil

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil. Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck.
