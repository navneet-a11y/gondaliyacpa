Deciding Whether Your Year Is Nil

The return is nil where the corporation had no revenue, no expenses, no income of any kind and no disposition of property in the tax year. Amount does not come into it.

A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil. Those are handled by Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year.

The entries that most often take a year out of nil are bank interest credited to a corporate account and a registry fee paid from it. The full test is in the guide to zero-activity corporate tax returns.

Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation Status

Most small Canadian corporations are Canadian-controlled private corporations, and the status matters on a nil return for one reason: it is a condition of the T2 Short.

The status turns on who controls the corporation and whether its shares are publicly traded. A corporation controlled by non-residents or by a public corporation does not have it.

Where the status is absent the return is a full T2 rather than the short form, and the figures reported are the same.

The Annual Return Is a Separate Filing

The annual return is filed with the registry the corporation was incorporated under. It confirms the directors, the registered office and the corporation's continued existence. It reports no income and it is not a tax return.

Missing it is what gets a corporation struck from a registry, which is a different and more abrupt problem than an unfiled T2.

It is not covered by the fee on this page. See the guide to provincial and federal annual returns.

Records a Small Corporation Keeps

Books and records are kept whether or not the corporation traded, and a nil year still has records: the bank statements that show no movement, the registry filings and the minute book.

A nil T2 does not require prepared financial statements. The balance sheet and income statement are reported through the coded GIFI format on the return itself.

See whether financial statements are needed.

The First Year After Incorporating

The first tax year runs from incorporation to the chosen year-end and may not exceed 53 weeks. The first return filed is what fixes the year-end, and Schedule 24 appears on it.

Many first years are nil, because the corporation was set up before trading began. A nil first year is filed exactly as any other.

See the guide to first-year T2 filing and when the first T2 is due.

Dormant Years Between Active Ones

A corporation that traded, stopped and may trade again files a nil return for each quiet year. The earlier trading does not change what the quiet year is.

Each year is assessed on its own facts, so a nil year sitting between two trading years is still a nil year.

See the guide to dormant and inactive corporations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Does a Small Corporation Have to File Every Year?

The T2 corporate income tax return, always. In most jurisdictions an annual return with the registry as well, and separately the Alberta AT1 or the Quebec CO-17 where the corporation is in those provinces.

Is the Annual Return the Same as the T2?

No. The annual return is a registry filing confirming the directors, the registered office and the corporation's existence. It reports no income and missing it is what gets a corporation struck from a registry.

Do I Need Financial Statements for a Nil T2?

No. The balance sheet and income statement are reported through the coded GIFI format on the return itself, so no separately prepared statements are required.

Can I File a Nil T2 Myself?

A director can, but for tax years starting after 2023 electronic filing is mandatory for virtually all corporations, and a paper return draws a flat charge. Returns here are transmitted electronically by a registered EFILE electronic filer.

Does an Open GST/HST Account Matter on a Dormant Corporation?

Yes. While the account is open, GST/HST returns remain due including nil ones, separately from the T2. It is closed with Form RC145.

What Happens When the Corporation Starts Trading?

That whole year is a trading year, whatever month the revenue arrives in. Filing moves to Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year.
