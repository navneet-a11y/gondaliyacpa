Everything Happens Electronically

The return is transmitted rather than posted, and Form T183CORP is signed on a secure single-use screen rather than printed. The Canada Revenue Agency accepts an electronic signature on it.

Mandatory T2 electronic filing reaches tax years beginning after 2023, so a corporation registered now is inside it from its very first return.

Signing the First Return

Form T183CORP authorises the transmission, and a signing officer of the corporation signs it before the return goes. On a brand new corporation that is usually the sole director.

It is signed once per tax year and per return, so catching up several years means one form for each. It is kept rather than filed, and produced if the Canada Revenue Agency asks.

The three first-year rules. The first tax year cannot exceed 53 weeks and the year-end must match CRA's record or the transmission is rejected. Schedule 24, the first-time filer schedule, is required on the first T2 a corporation ever files. The year-end you report is the one the Canada Revenue Agency holds on file, so a different date on the return is refused rather than corrected.

The Order It Happens In

  1. Confirm whether Newfoundland and Labrador wants a corporate return of its own, because that decides how many returns the first year needs.
  2. Confirm the year-end the Canada Revenue Agency holds, because the return is matched against it.
  3. Check the first tax year against the 53-week limit.
  4. Complete Schedule 24, which the first return always carries.
  5. Prepare the nil GIFI schedules for the year: Schedule 100, Schedule 125 and Schedule 141.
  6. Sign Form T183CORP for that tax year on a secure single-use screen.
  7. Transmit the return and keep the confirmation.
