Nil T2 filing in Canada › How Do I Find Out Which T2 Years Are Outstanding?

Your corporate tax account in the Canada Revenue Agency's My Business Account lists every tax year and whether a return has been assessed. Correspondence from the Agency, including any demand to file, names the years it is asking for. The incorporation date sets the first year that can be outstanding.

How Do I Find Out Which T2 Years Are Outstanding?

Where to lookWhat it shows
My Business Account, corporation income taxEach tax year and its assessment status
Correspondence from the AgencyThe years named in a demand to file
Certificate of incorporationThe date the first tax year starts from
Fiscal year-end on recordWhere each later tax year ends

A true nil return carries no percentage late-filing penalty, because that penalty is a percentage of tax owing and the corporation owes $0.00. The unfiled year still stays open forever, invites a demand to file and an arbitrary assessment, blocks a clearance certificate and dissolution, and costs a non-resident-owned corporation $25.00 per day up to $2,500.00.

A year with no return on record is an outstanding year, whether or not the Canada Revenue Agency has written about it. Silence from the Agency is not confirmation that a year was filed.
