Nil T2 filing in Canada › Can I Dissolve My Corporation With Unfiled T2 Returns?

Not cleanly. The corporate registry and the Canada Revenue Agency are separate, so a registry may strike a corporation while its tax years remain outstanding. A clearance certificate cannot be issued until every return is filed, and the unfiled years stay due after dissolution.
