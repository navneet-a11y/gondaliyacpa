Nil T2 filing in Canada › Can I E-Sign a T183CORP?

Yes. The Canada Revenue Agency accepts an electronic signature on Form T183CORP, and has done since June 22, 2023. A signing officer of the corporation signs it before the return is transmitted. The form is never sent to the Canada Revenue Agency; your filer keeps it and produces it on request.

Form T183CORP is the authorization a corporation gives before someone else transmits its T2 return. Without a signed T183CORP the return is not transmitted, which makes it the last step before filing rather than paperwork that follows it.

What the Acceptance Rests On

This is the part worth knowing. The Canada Revenue Agency’s acceptance of an electronic signature on Form T183CORP is an administrative measure rather than enacted law. The measure proposed in the 2021 budget was not passed, so the Canada Revenue Agency extended its administrative position instead, and it is still pursuing a permanent one. Making it permanent needs a change in the legislation.

In practice the position is settled for filing today. It is worth re-checking rather than assuming it is fixed, which is what the last-reviewed date at the foot of this page is for.

What Signing Electronically Does Not Change

Who may sign. A signing officer of the corporation signs Form T183CORP. An electronic signature does not widen that to anyone else, and it does not let the person transmitting the return sign on the corporation’s behalf.

Where the form goes. Nowhere. It is not filed with the return and it is not sent to the Canada Revenue Agency separately. It is kept, and produced if the Canada Revenue Agency asks for it later.

How long it is kept. At least six years after filing. Where the signature is electronic, the certificate of completion is kept with it, which matters most when the form itself does not show a timestamp.
