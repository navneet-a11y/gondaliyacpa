The Rule Is About the Method, Not the Content

For tax years beginning after 2023, electronic filing of the T2 is mandatory for virtually all corporations. The change removed a threshold that had previously exempted smaller corporations, so the obligation now reaches almost every corporation that files a T2 at all.

It says nothing about what the return contains. A return with nothing in it is transmitted electronically exactly as a return with everything in it, which is why a dormant corporation is not outside the rule and never was.

That catches people out in a particular way. Posting a return feels like the careful option, and on a quiet year it is the expensive one: a flat charge does not get smaller because the corporation did less.

What the Paper Route Costs on a Quiet Year

The charge for filing on paper behaves differently from anything else a corporation with no activity can be charged:

  • A paper T2 draws a flat $1,000.00, and a flat amount does not depend on how much tax the corporation owes.
  • A separate $100.00 per-return charge applies to a preparer who files a corporate return on paper where electronic filing was required.
  • That per-return charge attaches to the person who prepared and filed the return, not to the corporation.
  • Nothing about having no activity removes the obligation or the charge for ignoring it.
  • Being dormant is not an exception, having no bank account is not an exception, and finding the software inconvenient is not an exception.
  • Earlier tax years are transmitted electronically too, so catching up on six unfiled years does not mean posting six paper returns.
  • Returns here are transmitted electronically by a registered EFILE electronic filer, so neither charge arises.

A dormant corporation that posts a paper T2 is exposed to the full $1,000.00 on a return reporting $0.00. It is the largest single amount a genuinely nil corporation can be charged, and it is the one it can incur by being careful in the wrong way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is electronic filing of the T2 mandatory?

For tax years beginning after 2023 it is mandatory for virtually all corporations. The earlier threshold that exempted smaller corporations was removed.

What does filing a paper T2 cost?

A flat $1,000.00, plus a separate $100.00 per-return charge on the preparer. Neither is a percentage of anything.

Does a nil return escape that?

No. The amount is flat, so a dormant corporation that posts a paper return reporting $0.00 is exposed to the full $1,000.00.

Who pays the $100.00 per-return charge?

The preparer who filed a corporate return on paper where electronic filing was required. It attaches to that person, not to the corporation.

Can I file on paper through this service?

No. We file electronically only. Every return is transmitted by a registered EFILE electronic filer.

Does the rule depend on what is in the return?

No. It is about the method, not the content. A return with nothing in it is transmitted exactly as a return with everything in it.

Is being dormant an exception?

No. Being dormant is not an exception, having no bank account is not an exception, and finding the software inconvenient is not an exception.

What are the real exceptions?

A small number of cases defined by what the corporation is: an insurer, a non-resident corporation in certain circumstances and a corporation reporting in a functional currency.

Can older unfiled years be filed electronically?

Yes. Earlier tax years go through the same channel, prepared oldest first, each on its own form for its own tax year at $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

How do I know an electronic return arrived?

A confirmation from the Canada Revenue Agency, emailed within 3 business days of transmission. There is no envelope and no posting date to argue about.

What happens if the transmission is refused?

A refused transmission is not a filed return. The Agency names what it would not accept, the return is corrected and sent again, and one corrected transmission is included in the fee.

Is anything checked before the return is sent?

Yes. It is prepared in certified software and validated against the Agency’s rules before it is transmitted.

Does Form T183CORP change because the return is electronic?

Form T183CORP is what makes an electronic transmission possible. It is signed by an authorized signing officer before anything is sent, and it is kept for six years.

Does electronic filing cost more than posting a return?

The fee here is $300.00 plus HST per tax year whichever way you look at it, and the paper route carries a flat $1,000.00 plus the $100.00 per-return charge on the preparer.
