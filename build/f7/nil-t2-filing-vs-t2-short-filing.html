One Names the Year, the Other Names the Form

Asking whether to choose nil T2 filing or T2 Short filing is like asking whether to choose a Tuesday or a short letter. The two words answer different questions. One is about what happened in the tax year. The other is about which sheet of paper the return goes on.

Nil T2 filing is what this service does: it files the corporate income tax return for a year in which the corporation did nothing. The T2 Short is a form the Canada Revenue Agency publishes, a two-page version of the T2 that a corporation may use where the year meets every one of eight conditions. A quiet year usually meets them, which is why the two are so often confused, but "usually" is not "always" and the two tests are separate.

Why the Confusion Is Reasonable

Most quiet years do qualify for the short return, so in practice the two travel together and it can look as though choosing one is choosing the other. It is not. The year is decided by what the corporation did. The form is decided by the eight conditions, tested against that same year.

The two tests also overlap at two points, which is worth knowing before you assume a quiet year is automatically a short-return year. A dividend paid or received, and not being a Canadian-controlled private corporation throughout the year, each fail one of the eight conditions. They also mean the year is not a nil year for this service at all. Those two facts decide more than the form.

Which Question You Are Actually Asking

Work out which of the two you need an answer to, because they are tested separately:

  • Did anything move through the corporation in the tax year? That decides whether the year is a nil year, and whether this service files it.
  • Does that year meet all eight conditions for the short return? That decides which form carries it, and nothing else.
  • A quiet year that fails a condition is still a nil year. It goes on the full T2 at the same fee.
  • A year with a loss can meet every condition without being quiet. The short return carries it, but it is not a nil year and this service does not price it.
  • Neither question is answered by preference. Both are decided by what is true of the corporation for that year.
  • Both are tested year by year. A corporation can qualify one year and not the next, even where nothing looks different at the year-end.

Choosing the short return because it is shorter is not a choice that exists. Where a condition fails, the full T2 is the return, and filing the short one anyway risks the transmission being rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is T2 Short filing cheaper than nil T2 filing?

No, and they are not two prices to compare. The fee is $300.00 plus applicable sales tax per tax year for the tax year, whichever of the two forms carries the return.

Which one should I ask for?

Neither is asked for. Tell us the years that were quiet and we file them. Which form each year goes on is worked out from the eight conditions.

Is a nil return always filed on the T2 Short?

No. It usually is, because a quiet year usually meets the conditions. A quiet year that fails one goes on the full T2 at the same fee.

Is the T2 Short only for nil years?

No. The condition is nil net income or a loss, so a year with a loss can use the short return without being a nil year.

What are the eight conditions?

They are listed in full on T2 Short vs Full T2, and the T2 Short eligibility checker tests all eight at once.

Which condition fails most often?

A dividend paid or received, and not being a Canadian-controlled private corporation throughout the year. Each also means the year is not a nil year for this service, so they decide more than the form.

Does the short return carry fewer schedules?

No. GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 are required where anything sits on the books, on either form. The short return is shorter, not lighter.

Does the fee change if the full T2 is needed?

No. The fee is $300.00 plus applicable sales tax per tax year either way. Which form the year goes on does not change what the work costs.

Can some of my years use one form and some the other?

Yes. Each tax year is tested on its own, so a corporation can be on the short return one year and the full T2 the next.

Is Form T183CORP needed on the short return?

Yes. An authorized signing officer signs Form T183CORP before the return is transmitted, on either form.

How long until the confirmation arrives?

Your CRA confirmation is emailed within 3 business days of transmission, on either form.

What happens if the short return is filed when a condition fails?

The return is the wrong one for that year, and the transmission risks being rejected. That is why the conditions are tested before the return is prepared rather than after.

My year had a loss. Is that a nil year?

Not necessarily. A loss means something happened. A nil year is one in which nothing moved through the corporation, and the nil T2 eligibility checker tests it.

Does using the short return change the deadline?

No. The return is due six months after the end of the tax year on either form.

Which tool should I run first?

The nil T2 eligibility checker, which says whether the year is nil. The T2 Short eligibility checker then says which form carries it.
