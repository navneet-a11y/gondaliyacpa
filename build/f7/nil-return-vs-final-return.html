One Return, Two Points in a Corporation’s Life

A nil return and a final return are not two services. They are the same corporate income tax return filed for two different tax years: one while the corporation is still on the register and quiet, and one for the year it is wound up.

Which one applies is decided by where the corporation stands at the end of the year, not by anything you choose. A corporation that stopped trading but has not dissolved is still carrying on for filing purposes, so it files a nil return every year until the dissolution happens, and only then a final one.

What Decides Which One You Are Filing

The corporation’s own position at the end of the tax year decides it:

  • The corporation is on the register and did nothing in the year. That year is a nil return.
  • The corporation is being wound up. Its last tax year ends on the date of dissolution, and that return is the final one.
  • Every year before the final one is still its own return, filed on its own, at the same fee.
  • A corporation that stopped trading but has not dissolved is still carrying on for filing purposes. It files a nil return every year.
  • Unfiled earlier years block a clearance certificate, and they block the dissolution itself.
  • The final return does not dissolve the corporation on its own. The dissolution is done with the registry the corporation is incorporated under.

A corporation that stops trading and simply stops filing is not finished with its returns. It stays on the register, and every year it does not file stays open until that return is filed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a final return cost more than a nil return?

No. Both are $300.00 plus HST per tax year. The fee is counted per tax year, and the last year is charged the same as any other quiet year.

Does filing the final T2 dissolve the corporation?

No. Filing the final T2 does not dissolve the corporation on its own. The dissolution is done with the registry the corporation is incorporated under, and the return settles the corporation’s position with the Canada Revenue Agency.

What period does the final return cover?

The period that ends on the date of dissolution. It is dated from the dissolution rather than from the usual year-end, so the last period is often shorter than a full year.

When is the final return due?

Six months after the end of that final period, which is the same rule that sets the deadline for any other tax year.

Can the years before the final one be skipped?

No. Each year is its own return. Unfiled years block a clearance certificate and they block the dissolution itself, so in practice the final return is rarely the only one outstanding.

The corporation stopped trading years ago. Is each of those a nil return?

Yes, where nothing moved through it. A corporation that has stopped trading but has not dissolved is still carrying on for filing purposes, so every one of those years is a return at $300.00 plus HST per tax year.

What is a clearance certificate?

It is the Canada Revenue Agency’s confirmation of the corporation’s position before its property is distributed. It is requested separately from the return, and unfiled years block it.

Does a final return still need the GIFI schedules?

Yes, where anything sits on the books. The schedules follow what the corporation holds, not which year of its life the return covers.

Is Form T183CORP needed on a final return?

Yes. An authorized signing officer signs Form T183CORP before the return is transmitted, on the final return exactly as on any other.

How long until the confirmation arrives?

Your CRA confirmation is emailed within 3 business days of transmission, on either return.

What if the corporation is dissolved before the returns are filed?

The returns are still owed for the years the corporation existed. Dissolving does not close the years behind it, which is why the unfiled years are cleared first.

Can the final return be filed on the T2 Short?

Yes, where the corporation meets every one of the eight conditions for that year. Which form carries the return is a separate question from which year it covers, and it is set out in T2 Short vs Full T2.

Is the price different if several years are filed at once?

No. The fee is $300.00 plus HST per tax year, counted per year, however many years are filed together.

What happens if we stop filing instead of dissolving?

The corporation stays on the register and every year it does not file stays open. Stopping is not the same as finishing, and the returns are still owed when the corporation is eventually wound up.

Which tool should I run first?

The dissolution readiness checker, which names what is still outstanding. The final T2 return due date calculator then dates the last return.
