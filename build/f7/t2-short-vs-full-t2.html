Quick Answer

Both are the same return at the same price. The T2 Short is a two-page version of the T2 that a corporation may use only where it meets every one of the Canada Revenue Agency’s eight conditions for the short return. Where one condition fails, the full T2 is the return instead. Either way the filing is $300.00 plus HST per tax year, carries the same schedules, is signed on the same Form T183CORP and is transmitted the same way.

Two Forms, One Return

The T2 Short is not a lighter obligation or a cheaper service. It is a shorter version of the same corporate income tax return, and a corporation either qualifies to use it or does not. Nothing about the decision is ours to make and nothing about it changes what you pay.

That is the whole answer, and it is worth stating plainly because the form is often discussed as though choosing it were a strategy. It is not a choice. The eight conditions decide it, and where one of them fails we file the full return at the same fee rather than a different one.

The Eight Conditions Decide It

A corporation may use the short return only where every one of these holds for the tax year:

  • The corporation is a Canadian-controlled private corporation throughout the tax year.
  • It has nil net income, or a loss, for income tax purposes this year.
  • It has one permanent establishment only.
  • It paid no taxable dividends and received none in the year.
  • It reports in Canadian currency.
  • It is claiming no refundable credits.
  • It has no Ontario transitional tax debit.
  • It has no amount calculated under section 34.2.

Meeting seven of the eight is not enough. One condition failing puts the corporation on the full return, which we file at the same fee rather than a different one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the T2 Short cheaper than the full T2?

No. Both are $300.00 plus HST per tax year. The T2 Short is included in the fee where a corporation qualifies for it rather than charged separately, and where it does not qualify we file the full return at the same fee.

Can I choose to file the T2 Short?

It is not a choice. A corporation may use the short return only where it meets every one of the eight conditions. Meeting seven is not enough, and we check all eight before the return is prepared.

What is actually different between the two forms?

Two things: which corporations may use it, and how long the form is. The deadline, the schedules, Form T183CORP, the filing route and the confirmation are the same on both.

Does the short form mean fewer schedules?

No. The schedules are decided by what sits on the corporation’s books and by whether it is a first return, not by which form carries them. A quiet corporation holding share capital still files GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 either way.

Which condition fails most often?

A dividend paid or received, and not being a Canadian-controlled private corporation throughout the year. Both also mean the year is not a nil year for this service, so they decide more than the form.

Who decides which form my return goes on?

The eight conditions do, tested against your corporation before the return is prepared. The T2 Short eligibility checker runs the same test you can run yourself.
