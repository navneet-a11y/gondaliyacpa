A Price on Its Own Answers One Question

A published price tells a reader what a provider charges. It does not tell them what arrives for the money, who does the work, or what happens when a year turns out not to be nil, and those are usually the questions that decide the choice.

This page is the plainest case of that on the site. One figure was recorded on the date above and nothing else was, so eight cells of the right-hand column say the published price does not state it. Each of those cells is deliberate wording: it says what the record holds, not what the provider does.

So the honest use of this page is to read the left-hand column closely. Every line of it is on this site and can be checked, and the section below sets the whole of it out.

What the Fee Here Covers

Since the comparison is one described price against one undescribed figure, here is the description in full:

  • The T2 itself, and GIFI Schedules 100, 125 and 141 where the corporation has anything to report.
  • Schedule 50 for any shareholder at 10% or more, Schedule 24 on a first return, and Schedule 8 with loss continuity carried forward intact.
  • Preparation and review by a CPA Ontario member before anything is transmitted.
  • Transmission by a registered EFILE electronic filer, prepared and transmitted in Canada, with credentials never shared and never used offshore.
  • Form T183CORP signed on a secure screen, and the CRA confirmation emailed within 3 business days.
  • One price for one tax year for one corporation, with no tier above or below it.

A year that turns out not to be nil is not covered by this fee. A holding corporation with investment income, a rental corporation and a corporation that paid or received a dividend are not nil. Those are handled by Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation at gondaliyacpa.ca at $350.00 plus HST per year.
