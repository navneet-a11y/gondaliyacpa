One Screen, Start to Finish

Nil T2 filing here is a form, not an appointment. You type your corporation name, we fill in what the Canada Revenue Agency already knows, you pick the unfiled years and confirm there was no activity in each, and you pay. We prepare the return, a CPA reviews it, you sign Form T183CORP, and we transmit it. The fee is $300.00 plus applicable sales tax per tax year.

No meeting. No booking link. No email chain. One screen, start to finish.

The Ten Steps, In Order

  1. Type your corporation name.
  2. We fill in your CRA Business Number and incorporation date.
  3. Pick the tax years you have not filed.
  4. Confirm the corporation had no activity.
  5. Attest that you are an authorized signing officer and pass the identity check.
  6. Pay by credit card, debit, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  7. We prepare the return, generate your Form T183CORP, and send you a single-use signing link.
  8. You sign on a secure access-controlled screen.
  9. A CPA reviews it and files it electronically.
  10. Your CRA confirmation arrives by email within 3 business days.

The order is fixed. Nothing in it is skipped and nothing is reordered, because each step depends on the one before it: the return cannot be prepared before the years are chosen, and Form T183CORP cannot carry the return’s figures before the return exists.

What Each Step Is Doing

Steps one and two exist so you do not have to find your Business Number or your incorporation date. Steps three and four are the only ones where you supply anything we cannot look up, and they are the two that decide whether the return is nil at all.

Step five is the attestation and the identity check. Step seven is where Form T183CORP is generated, and it carries that return’s figures, which is why it cannot be signed in advance. Step eight is a secure access-controlled screen reached by a single-use link, and your signature carries a date and a timestamp.

Step nine is the CPA review, and step ten is the Canada Revenue Agency’s confirmation, which arrives by email within 3 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday.

What It Costs

$300.00 plus applicable sales tax per tax year. Each tax year is a separate return and carries the same fee, so six unfiled years is six returns. The sales tax that applies where your corporation is resident is added at checkout. See the full nil T2 filing cost, or the list of what the fee does not cover.

If Your Corporation Turns Out Not To Be Nil

This service is for a corporation with no activity in the tax year. A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil. Other cases that are not nil include a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or a corporation already dissolved or struck.
