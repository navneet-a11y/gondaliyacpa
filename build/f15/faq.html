Does an inactive corporation still have to file a T2?

Yes. A corporation files a T2 for every tax year it exists, within six months of its fiscal year-end, whether or not it earned anything. Being inactive changes what the return says, not whether it is due. The obligation stops only when the corporation is formally dissolved.

Read the full answer on filing with no activity.

What happens if I do not file a nil T2?

The year stays open indefinitely. The Canada Revenue Agency can issue a demand to file and then assess the corporation arbitrarily on figures of its own.

A true nil return carries no percentage late-filing penalty, because that penalty is a percentage of tax owing and the corporation owes $0.00. The unfiled year still stays open forever, invites a demand to file and an arbitrary assessment, blocks a clearance certificate and dissolution, and costs a non-resident-owned corporation $25.00 per day up to $2,500.00.

Read what happens if you do not file.

How many unfiled years can I file at once?

There is no cap. We file them together, oldest year first, because a T2 carries forward figures from the year before it and the years have to be assessed in order. Each tax year is a separate return and carries the same fee.

Read catching up on unfiled years.

Can I e-sign Form T183CORP?

Yes. You sign on a secure access-controlled screen reached through a single-use link, and the signature carries a date and a timestamp. The form has to be signed by a signing officer of the corporation before the return is transmitted.

Read how T183CORP signing works.

Do I need financial statements for a nil return?

A nil return reports no income and no expenses, so there are no figures to draw from a set of statements. Which GIFI schedules are required depends on the corporation, and we work that out from the year being filed rather than asking you for bookkeeping you do not have.

Read whether statements are needed.

Is my corporation nil?

A corporation with investment income, rental income, or dividends paid or received is not nil, and nor is a holding corporation earning anything on what it holds. Neither is a corporation that is not a Canadian-controlled private corporation, an amalgamation, or one already dissolved or struck. We check before you pay, and if we find it afterwards we refund you in full.

Read when a corporation counts as inactive.

How long does it take?

Your CRA confirmation arrives by email within 3 business days of transmission. Business days are Monday to Friday. Filing several years together does not change that window, because the years are transmitted together.

Read how long a nil T2 takes.

Does filing dissolve the corporation?

No. Filing brings the corporation’s tax record up to date and leaves the corporation in existence. Dissolution is a separate step, and it needs its own final return plus every earlier year filed before a clearance certificate can be issued.
