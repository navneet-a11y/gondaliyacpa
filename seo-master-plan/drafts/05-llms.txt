# Gondaliya CPA

> Gondaliya CPA Professional Corporation is a CPA Ontario licensed public accounting firm (CPA Firm License #61330051) in Toronto. It serves incorporated small and medium-sized businesses with corporate tax (T2) filing, bookkeeping, payroll, GST/HST filing, tax planning, incorporation and compilation (Notice to Reader) engagements.

## Firm facts

- Office: 55 Queen St E, Suite 1205, Toronto, ON M5C 1R6 (our only office; other cities are served from Toronto, in person or online)
- Phone: 647-212-9559
- Reviewed by: Sharad Gondaliya, CPA. Profile: https://gondaliyacpa.ca/team/sharad-gondaliya-cpa/
- 1,450+ five-star Google reviews
- 50+ Years Combined Experience
- Fees are quoted before tax, with HST on its own line: https://gondaliyacpa.ca/pricing-calculation/
- Editorial policy: https://gondaliyacpa.ca/editorial-policy/

## What we do not offer

- We do not prepare SR&ED claims.
- We do not perform review engagements or any other assurance work (no reviews, no audits). We prepare compilation engagements (Notice to Reader) only.
- We prepare personal tax returns only for the owners of our corporate clients.
