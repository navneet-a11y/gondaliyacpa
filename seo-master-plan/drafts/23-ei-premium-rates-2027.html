EI Premium Rates 2027: Employer and Employee Guide

Written and reviewed by Sharad Gondaliya, CPA · Last reviewed: [DATE OF CPA APPROVAL] · CPA Firm License — #61330051 · Editorial policy

Short answer: For 2027, employees outside Quebec pay EI premiums of $1.64 per $100 of insurable earnings, up to maximum insurable earnings of $70,800. That is a maximum of $1,161.12 per employee. Employers pay 1.4 times the employee premium: $2.296 per $100, up to $1,625.57 per employee.

2027 EI premium rates at a glance

Parameter2027 (outside Quebec)
Employee premium rate$1.64 per $100 of insurable earnings (1.64%)
Employer premium rate$2.296 per $100 of insurable earnings (1.4 times the employee rate)
Maximum insurable earnings$70,800
Maximum annual employee premium$1,161.12
Maximum annual employer premium, per employee$1,625.57

Employees who live in Quebec pay a lower EI rate because Quebec runs its own parental insurance plan. Use the Quebec rate that CRA publishes for 2027 for those employees.

Both the rate and the earnings ceiling go up in 2027. For an employee earning $70,800 or more, the employer pays $53.27 more in EI for 2027 than for 2026. The 2026 figures are on our 2026 EI premium guide.

Once an employee's insurable earnings for the calendar year pass $70,800, no more EI is deducted for that employee for the rest of the year.

How EI is worked out on each paycheque

StepExample: $5,000 bi-weekly gross pay
1. Take the insurable earnings for the pay period$5,000, all insurable
2. Apply the employee rate of 1.64%$5,000 × 1.64% = $82.00 deducted from the employee
3. Employer premium is 1.4 times the employee premium$82.00 × 1.4 = $114.80
4. Stop at the annual maximumAfter 14 pays the employee has paid $1,148.00. The 15th pay deducts only $13.12, which brings the total to $1,161.12. Nothing more is deducted after that.
5. Remit both amounts to CRA$82.00 + $114.80 = $196.80 for the pay period, by your remittance due date
