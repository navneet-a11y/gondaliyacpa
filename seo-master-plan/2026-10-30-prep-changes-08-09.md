## 9A. Calculator (35514): drop-in replacement

The drop-in is `seo-master-plan/drafts/09-corporate-tax-calculator.html`. It replaces two blocks of the widget:
- the `<!-- CALCULATOR -->` markup, from `<div style="padding: 0 20px;" id="ctc-calculator">` to its closing `</div>`
- the `<!-- CALCULATOR JAVASCRIPT -->` script

It keeps every existing class and id, and adds:
- `#ctc-yearend`, a date input defaulting to 2026-12-31
- result rows `#r-period`, `#r-limit` and `#r-ont-sbd-label`
- a relabelled taxable capital input. The `#ctc-capital` input already existed.

**Logic:**
- The taxation year is 12 months ending on the year-end.
- The Ontario SBD rate is (3.2% × days before July 1, 2026 + 2.2% × days on or after) ÷ days in the year.
- Federal 9% / 15%, Ontario general 11.5%.
- Business limit = 500,000 × (50M − capital) ÷ 40M, clamped between $0 and $500,000.
- Helpers are renamed `ctcFmt` and `ctcPct` to avoid global `fmt` clashes.

**Bug fixed in the old code.** The old grind was `(capital − 10M) × 0.225`, so any capital of about $12.2M or more wiped out the limit. At $30M the old code gave a $0 limit; the correct limit is $250,000.

**Footer line inside the block.** The original `flat-fee from $400.` was changed to `flat-fee from $400 before tax (HST shown on its own line).` Revert it if the fee wording should go through its own change.
