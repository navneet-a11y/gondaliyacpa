## Method and cut-off

- **Data:** Search Console page data for 5 Aug to 2 Nov 2026 (90 days). URLs with #fragments or ?query strings were folded into their path, with clicks and impressions added together and position weighted by impressions. A page that is not in the file had 0 impressions.
- **Cut-off (as agreed):** 100 or more impressions = KEEP; under 100 = MERGE.
- **Inventory:** I pulled all 1,266 published pages and 511 published posts from WordPress, read-only. That gave 289 pages whose slug starts with "accountant-for-". One of them, /accountant-for-truck-drivers-trucking-companies/, is the original trucking page, so I treated it as a hub, which leaves 288 niche pages. All 288 are linked from /industries/.
- **Extras outside the "accountant-for-" pattern:**
  - No "tax-accountant-for-" pages exist.
  - /incorporated-contractor-accountant-toronto/ is a one-off niche page. It is used here as a hub.
  - About 90 "service + industry" pages are left out of scope: gst-hst-filing-for-, bookkeeping-for-, payroll-services-for-, catch-up-…-for-, cfo-services-for-, business-plan-writing-service-for- and similar.
- **Original industry pages (hubs, never merge candidates):** the 78 pages linked from /industries/ that are not "accountant-for-" pages, for example /healthcare/, /dentists/, /construction/, /restaurants/, /e-commerce/ and /tech-companies/. /industries/ currently has 19 categories and 367 links. The 42 hubs follow those categories but split the very large ones.
- **Niche posts:** I matched each post to a niche page by the distinctive words in its slug, leaving out generic words such as tax, canada, accounting, guide and deductions. I then checked every match by hand. Posts from before 24 Aug, which belong to the original pages such as mortgage brokers, manufacturing and dentists, and the general non-resident and PSB series, are not niche posts and were left out.
  - Posts with any impressions: KEEP-POST, left as they are.
  - Posts with zero impressions: MERGE, redirected to their niche page's hub, or to the niche page itself if that page is kept.
- **Redirects:** every MERGE row has exactly one target in redirect_to. KEEP rows have a hub for /industries/ linking only.
