## The angle — what makes this page different from everything near it

This is the only **corporate and outbound** page in category 18. The three siblings built beside
it (US citizens living in Canada, green card holders in Canada, Canadians working in the US) are
all individual and inbound. Two pages already on the site sit close and must not be restated:
`/us-corporation-llc-tax-filing/` covers return-filing mechanics, and
`/accountant-for-foreign-shareholders-of-canadian-corporations/` covers inbound foreign ownership.
This page earns its place on **the structuring decision a Canadian corporation makes before it
puts people, contracts or a subsidiary into the United States** — not on filing a return and not
on inbound ownership.

The lead argument is the **US LLC trap**: CRA generally treats a US LLC as a corporation while the
US treats it as fiscally transparent, so the two systems can disagree about who earned the income
and who paid the tax, which is how foreign tax credits get lost. That mismatch is modelled before
the entity is formed, not discovered afterwards.
