Four subjects are specific to a streamer and appear on none of them:

1. **Several different kinds of money, taxed by different logic.** Subscriptions, bits, ads,
   sponsorships, affiliate links and viewer donations are not one revenue line. The donation
   question in particular — whether a viewer tip is a gift or business income — is the question
   streamers ask most and get answered worst.
2. **The payer is foreign.** Twitch pays from the United States, which puts **Form W-8BEN** and the
   treaty in play before any money arrives, and a foreign tax credit under **ITA section 126** in
   play if tax was withheld anyway.
3. **Hobby or business.** A streamer with forty viewers and a new PC is in a genuinely different
   position from one with a contract, and the test is whether there is a source of income at all.
   This decides whether the losses are deductible.
4. **The gear and the room.** A streaming setup is a capital asset mixed with personal use, run out
   of a bedroom, and the business-use-of-home limit in **ITA subsection 18(12)** caps the deduction
   at the income from the business.
