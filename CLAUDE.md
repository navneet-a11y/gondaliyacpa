## Hard rules — never violate these in any page, schema, sitemap, image or social post

1. About CRA, the **only** permitted wording is "registered EFILE electronic filer".
   Never *authorized*, *agent*, *approved*, *partner*, *endorsed*. No CRA logos or wordmarks.
2. Always write **T183CORP**, never T183.
3. Never write "no penalty" (or *penalty free*, *zero penalty*) standing alone. It always
   carries the full qualification block from Section 7.4.
4. Never claim cheapest, best, fastest, guaranteed, risk-free. Competitor pages carry only
   the published price and the date it was captured.
5. The EFILE number and the CRA GroupID never appear anywhere — body, schema, sitemap,
   image, email or social.
6. No review/aggregateRating schema, no FAQPage, no HowTo. Trustindex rich snippets stay off.
7. No booking widget, no Calendly, no "book a call" anywhere. `/consultation/` only explains
   the hand-off to gondaliyacpa.ca at $350 including HST per year.
8. Holding, rental and investment pages always state such a corporation is **not** nil.
   Quebec pages state CO-17 is a separate return; Alberta pages say the same for AT1.
9. Every price reads **$300 including HST per tax year**.
10. Staging stays noindexed. Live stays noindexed until Sharad Gondaliya confirms launch.
    Build to the dates regardless.

The complete banned-phrases table with approved replacements is Section 7.4 of the plan.
No emoji anywhere, including titles, meta descriptions and social copy.
