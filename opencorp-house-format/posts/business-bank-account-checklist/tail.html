Quick Answers: Key Numbers at a Glance

At a Glance
  • Beneficial owner threshold: 25% or more of shares or votes
  • ISC register: Required under the CBCA and OBCA
  • Photo ID: Every signing officer and every 25% owner
  • Business number: Issued with Ontario and federal incorporation
  • Apostille: Accepted for many foreign documents since January 11, 2024
  • Corporate profile report: From the Ontario Business Registry or Corporations Canada
  • Our fee: $35 including HST, plus government fees

Who This Is For / Not For

Fit Check
  • For: Owners of a newly incorporated Ontario or federal corporation who want to walk into the bank once with everything in hand.
  • Not For: Businesses that need regulated trust accounts, such as law firms, which follow extra rules from their regulator.
Verdict

Most account delays come from paperwork, not the bank. Bring the certificate, articles, corporate profile, a signed banking resolution, current registers including the ISC register, and ID for every signer and 25% owner, with names matching the registry exactly. Then fund the account with a documented share subscription or shareholder loan.

2026 Update

2026 Update — what is current: This article reflects rules current to November 2026. Banks must identify anyone who owns or controls 25% or more of a corporation under FINTRAC's beneficial ownership rules. Federal and Ontario corporations must keep an ISC register, and federal corporations also file ISC information with Corporations Canada. Canada has accepted apostilles since January 11, 2024. Ontario and federal incorporations still receive a business number automatically.
