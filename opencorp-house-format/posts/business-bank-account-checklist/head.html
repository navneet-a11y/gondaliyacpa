Quick Summary

Banks open a corporate account once they can see that the corporation exists, who may sign for it and who owns it. Bring the certificate and articles, a current corporate profile, a banking resolution, your three registers, photo ID for every signer and every 25% owner, and proof of the registered office. Names must match the registry exactly.
