Essential Documents Needed to Open a Corporate Bank Account
To get started, you usually need these important papers:

Certificate of Incorporation: This shows your corporation is legally registered, either federally or provincially.
Articles of Incorporation: This document lays out how your company is set up and what it does.
CRA Business Number (BN): The Canada Revenue Agency gives this number to identify your business for taxes.
Government-Issued Identification: You’ll need valid photo ID like a driver’s license or passport for all owners and anyone who can sign on the account.
Keep in mind, each bank may have its own specific list because of rules from PCMLTFA and FINTRAC. Always check with your bank before applying.

Corporate Banking Checklist: What You Should Prepare Before Applying
Besides incorporation papers, banks want extra info about who runs the company and where it operates. Here’s what you should have ready:

Directors’ Register: A current list of people who manage the corporation.
Shareholders’ Register: Records showing who owns shares and how much.
Individual with Significant Control (ISC) or Beneficial Ownership Information: Info about anyone owning 25% or more, as per FINTRAC rules.
Proof of Registered Office Address: Paperwork that confirms your official business address filed with Corporations Canada or provincial registry.
Having these ready helps avoid delays when banks check identities for anti-money laundering laws.

FAQs on Corporate Bank Account Setup and Compliance

What is a corporate profile report and why do banks require it?
A corporate profile report shows your company’s official details. Banks use it to verify incorporation status and director information.

What are by-laws or banking resolutions in relation to bank accounts?
By-laws or banking resolutions authorize who can open and operate the bank account. Banks require these documents to confirm signers’ authority.

How important are shareholder registers for corporate banking?
Shareholder registers list owners and share distribution. Banks review them to identify beneficial owners and comply with FINTRAC rules.

What is an ISC register and who must be included?
An ISC (Individuals with Significant Control) register tracks people owning 25% or more shares or voting rights. It ensures transparency under PCMLTFA laws.

Can non-resident directors open a corporate bank account in Canada?
Yes, but banks may ask for notarized ID or apostilled foreign documents to verify identity under FINTRAC guidelines.

What role does a share subscription agreement play in banking?
This agreement confirms how shares were issued and paid for. Banks may request it to verify ownership legitimacy.

Why do banks ask for a shareholder loan agreement?
It clarifies loans from shareholders to the corporation. Banks use this to assess financial obligations and compliance with tax rules.

What is a minute book, and do banks require it?
A minute book stores all corporate records, including resolutions. Some banks request it as proof of proper governance.

How does banking resolution drafting help in account setup?
Properly drafted resolutions ensure authorized persons can transact. It avoids delays during bank approval processes.

What is post-incorporation setup CPA support?
CPA support helps organize documents, register accounts, and advise on compliance after incorporation for smooth banking relations.

How does legal drafting of resolutions affect banking approvals?
Well-drafted resolutions meet bank policies for authorization. Poorly prepared documents cause rejections or delays.

What are business account funding entries?
These are initial deposits made when opening the account. Banks record them to confirm active business operations.
