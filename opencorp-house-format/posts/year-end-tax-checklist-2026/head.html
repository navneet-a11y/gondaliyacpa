Quick Summary

For a December 31, 2026 year-end, pay any dividends and declare any bonus before December 31. Then work through the 2027 dates: payroll on January 15, T4 and T5 slips and RRSP contributions on March 1, the balance of tax on March 31 for most CCPCs, an accrued bonus by June 29, and the T2 and Ontario annual return by June 30.

Scope & Assumptions

This article covers Canadian-controlled private corporations in Ontario with a December 31, 2026 year-end, using CRA limits for 2026. Corporations with a different year-end move every date by the same number of months. This is educational information only and not tax, legal, or financial advice.
