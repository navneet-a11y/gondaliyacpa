Who This Is For / Not For

Fit Check
  • For: Owner-managers of Ontario corporations with a December 31 year-end who decide their own salary, bonus and dividends.
  • Not For: Corporations with a non-calendar year-end, which shift each date, and corporations with associated companies or large passive portfolios, which need a full review.
Verdict

Do three things before December 31: pay any 2026 dividends, put any bonus in a signed resolution, and make sure new equipment is available for use. Then diarize the 2027 dates, because most year-end penalties come from missed January and March deadlines rather than from the December decisions themselves.
