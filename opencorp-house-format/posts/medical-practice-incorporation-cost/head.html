Quick Summary

A physician professional corporation costs less to set up than most doctors expect. Government and college fees in the first year come to $725 in Ontario, $730 in British Columbia and $800 in Alberta plus its NUANS report. The college renewal then runs $175 a year in Ontario, $135 in BC and $200 plus GST in Alberta.
