Quick Answers: Key Numbers at a Glance

At a Glance
  • CPSO certificate of authorization: $400 new, $175 each year
  • CPSBC corporation permit: $350 new, $135 each year
  • CPSA professional corporation: $500 plus GST new, $200 plus GST each year per physician shareholder
  • Ontario Business Registry: $300 to incorporate
  • BC Registries: $350 to incorporate, plus a $30 Name Request
  • Alberta registry: $275 government fee
  • Small business deduction: First $500,000 of active business income
  • Passive income threshold: $50,000 a year
  • Our fee: $35 including HST, plus government and college fees

Who This Is For / Not For

Fit Check
  • For: Licensed physicians in Ontario, British Columbia or Alberta who earn more than they spend and want to budget the setup and yearly cost of a professional corporation.
  • Not For: Medical students and residents without an independent licence, and physicians in Quebec, who follow Collège des médecins du Québec rules not covered in depth here.
Verdict

The fees are modest: $725 in Ontario, $730 in British Columbia and $800 in Alberta in the first year, then $135 to $200 plus GST a year for the college. The real question is income. Incorporation pays when you can leave money in the corporation; if you spend everything you bill, the yearly T2 and bookkeeping cost more than the deferral saves.

2026 Update

2026 Update — what is current: This article reflects fees and rules current to November 2026. The CPSO charges $400 for a new certificate of authorization and $175 to renew. The CPSBC charges $350 for a new permit and $135 a year. The CPSA charges $500 plus GST to register and $200 plus GST a year per physician shareholder. Ontario charges $300 to incorporate, BC $350 and Alberta $275. The small business deduction still covers $500,000 of active business income and is ground down once passive income passes $50,000.
