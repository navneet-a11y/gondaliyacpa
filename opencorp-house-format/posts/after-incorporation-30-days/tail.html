Quick Answers: Key Numbers at a Glance

At a Glance
  • Ontario initial return: Within 60 days of incorporation
  • Notice of change: Within 15 days
  • GST/HST registration: Once taxable sales pass $30,000 in four calendar quarters
  • Payroll remittance: By the 15th of the month after you pay
  • WSIB registration: Within 10 days of hiring your first worker in a covered industry
  • Federal annual return: Within 60 days after the anniversary date
  • Ontario annual return: Within six months after year-end
  • T2 return: Six months after year-end
  • Our fee: $35 including HST, plus government fees

Who This Is For / Not For

Fit Check
  • For: Owners of a newly incorporated Ontario or federal corporation who want a clear list of what to do in the first month.
  • Not For: Corporations moving an existing business or assets in, which need tax advice on the transfer before anything else.
Verdict

The certificate is the start, not the finish. In the first 30 days, sign the organizational resolutions, issue the shares, start the minute book, confirm your CRA accounts and open a corporate bank account. Then diarize three dates: the Ontario initial return within 60 days, any notice of change within 15 days, and your first annual return.

2026 Update

2026 Update — what is current: This article reflects rules current to November 2026. Ontario corporations, and federal corporations operating in Ontario, file an initial return within 60 days. Changes of directors or registered office are reported within 15 days. Federal annual returns are due within 60 days after the anniversary date, Ontario annual returns within six months after year-end, and T2 returns six months after year-end. The GST/HST small supplier threshold remains $30,000. Federal corporations have filed individuals with significant control information with Corporations Canada since January 22, 2024, and Ontario corporations keep an ISC register under OBCA s.140.2.
