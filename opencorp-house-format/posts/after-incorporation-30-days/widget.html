First 30 Days Readiness Check

Six quick questions. No tax calculation, no figure quoted.

1. Have the organizational resolutions been signed?
2. Have shares been issued to the owners?
3. Do you have a minute book?
4. Will you pay employees in the next 30 days?
5. Do you expect more than $30,000 in taxable sales within a year?
6. Is the corporate bank account open?
