Business Number Readiness Check

Six quick questions. No tax calculation, no figure quoted.

1. Do you know your incorporation number?
2. Do you know your 9-digit business number?
3. Do you expect more than $30,000 in taxable sales within a year?
4. Will you pay employees, including yourself by salary?
5. Will you import or export goods?
6. Do your invoices show the right number?
