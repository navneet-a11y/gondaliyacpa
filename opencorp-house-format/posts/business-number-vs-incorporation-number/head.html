Quick Summary

A corporation has two separate identifiers. The incorporation number comes from Corporations Canada or the provincial registry and proves the corporation exists. The 9-digit business number comes from the CRA and anchors every tax account. Your GST/HST number is not a third number: it is the business number plus RT0001.
