Quick Answers: Key Numbers at a Glance

At a Glance
  • Business number: 9 digits, issued by the CRA
  • GST/HST number: BN + RT0001
  • Corporate income tax account: BN + RC0001
  • Payroll account: BN + RP0001
  • Import/export account: BN + RM0001
  • GST/HST registration: Once taxable sales pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters
  • Ontario corporation number: 10 digits for corporations formed since October 19, 2021
  • Which number goes on invoices: The GST/HST number, never the incorporation number
  • Our fee: $35 including HST, plus government fees

Who This Is For / Not For

Fit Check
  • For: New Ontario or federal corporation owners, and sole proprietors about to incorporate, who want to know which number to use where.
  • Not For: Registered charities and Quebec QST registrants, who have extra rules not covered in depth here.
Verdict

Keep the two numbers apart. Your incorporation number proves the corporation exists and belongs in the minute book and on registry filings. Your 9-digit business number belongs on everything the CRA sees, and with RT0001 added it is the GST/HST number that goes on your invoices.

2026 Update

2026 Update — what is current: This article reflects rules current to November 2026. The CRA business number is still 9 digits, with program accounts RC, RT, RP, RM, RZ and RR added as two letters and four digits. Ontario and federal incorporations still receive a business number automatically. The GST/HST small supplier threshold remains $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters. The Ontario Business Registry has issued 10-digit Ontario corporation numbers since October 19, 2021.
