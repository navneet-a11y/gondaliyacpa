Quick Summary

You can incorporate a side business while you keep your job, once your employment contract allows it. A corporation pays 12.2% on profit it keeps, but early losses stay trapped inside it and CPP is still due on any salary it pays you. For most employees it pays once steady profit passes about $50,000 a year that you can leave in the company.
