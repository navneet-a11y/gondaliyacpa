Verdict

For most founders the government cost is simple: $300 to incorporate in Ontario or $200 federally online, plus a NUANS report for a named company. After that, Ontario charges nothing a year at the registry, while a federal corporation pays $12 a year online. Choose the jurisdiction for where you will operate, not for the $100 difference.

2026 Update

2026 Update — what is current: This article reflects fees current to November 2026. The federal government charges $200 to incorporate online and $250 on paper, and Ontario charges $300 online. Federal annual returns cost $12 online and $40 by mail, Ontario annual returns are free, and director or address changes are free in both. Articles of amendment cost $150 in Ontario and $200 federally. Federal corporations need at least 25% resident Canadian directors, while Ontario has had no residency rule since July 5, 2021.
