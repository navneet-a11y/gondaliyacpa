## Page model

Each page follows the gondaliyacpa.ca model page: hero with the focus keyword, CPA and CFA byline with badges and review status, trust strip, at-a-glance box, key figures, calculators, table of contents, 10 to 14 numbered sections, gridline tables, a worked hypothetical example, FAQ with FAQPage schema (ItemList on lists, Review on reviews), the Meet the experts block with both bios, the figures-checked date and the disclaimer.

## Rules

- No links to other websites except credential and profile links on the two profile pages; internal links only to the 101 planned URLs.
- 43 calculators keep readers on the site (for example TFSA room from birth year and the year the reader became a Canadian tax resident).
- Every figure comes from a verified register entry or arithmetic re-run in Python; anything unconfirmed shows as "not published".
- General information only (no stock, ETF or coin picks); every Wealthsimple page says WealthScale is independent and not affiliated with Wealthsimple.
- Neha's line shows "Review of this page scheduled" until Navneet sets the ws_neha_reviewed_on field after her written sign-off.
