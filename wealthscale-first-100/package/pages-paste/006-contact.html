Use a clear subject line. Please start the subject with the reason for writing, such as "Correction", "Media" or "Privacy". It helps your message reach the right person sooner.

2. Report an Error or a Wrong Figure

What to send. To report an error, please email info@wealthscale.ca with three things:

  • The page address, copied from your browser.
  • The figure or statement that looks wrong, as it appears on the page.
  • Where you saw the correct one, such as a provider's current pricing, a notice from the Canada Revenue Agency or a government publication, with its date if it has one.

What happens next. We check the figure against its primary source. If the page is wrong, we correct it and update the page date. Every page ends with a line that says when its figures were last checked and against which type of source.

What counts as an error. A wrong or out-of-date rate, fee or limit; a rule that has changed; a calculation that does not add up; or a product feature that is described wrongly. A disagreement with a score is not an error, but the scoring rules are published in how we rate online brokers and robo-advisors.

The full policy. Our editorial standards explain the sources we use, how often each type of figure is re-checked and how corrections work.

Correcting your own pricing. If your fees, rates or account features have changed, please send the change and the date it took effect. We check it against your published pricing, the same way we check a reader's report.

6. What We Can and Cannot Help With

WealthScale is a publisher, not a bank, broker, dealer or accounting firm. That sets the limits on what we can do by email.

We can help with

  • Questions about something on one of our pages
  • Reports of a wrong or out-of-date figure
  • How a score or a cost comparison was worked out
  • Which of our calculators fits a question
  • Media enquiries
  • Partnership enquiries from providers
  • Privacy requests about information we hold

We cannot help with

  • Personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice
  • Telling you which stock, ETF, fund or crypto to buy
  • Choosing an account or provider for your situation
  • Opening, closing or accessing any account
  • Problems with an account at a bank, broker or platform
  • Preparing or filing a tax return
  • Questions about your own Canada Revenue Agency account

Why we cannot advise you. WealthScale provides general information only. We do not know your income, goals, tax position or tolerance for risk, so we cannot say what suits you. For advice on your own situation, please speak with a qualified professional who can review your facts.

Problems with an account. We cannot see, open or change any account. For a problem with an account, please contact the provider that holds it.
