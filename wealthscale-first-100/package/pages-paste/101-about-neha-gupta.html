Neha Gupta, CFA

CFA charterholder · Financial Reviewer, WealthScale · Head of Operations and Finance, Whiteland Real Estate, Winnipeg

Neha Gupta is a CFA charterholder based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. She has worked in financial markets since December 2005. She started as a trainee at Delta Investment Services in Surat, India, then worked there as an equity dealer and later as a manager handling client portfolios and training staff.

From 2013, Neha wrote and edited investment research full time. She has published more than 2,600 articles on stocks, ETFs, hedge fund filings, forex, crypto and commodities, including 1,534 for Market Realist, 798 for TipRanks and more than 285 for Insider Monkey. She has also written for Seeking Alpha and Investing in the Web. At WallstreetPR she hired, trained and supervised a team of financial writers. In 2015 she founded ResearchCows Financials, a financial research and writing firm.

Since November 2023, Neha has been Head of Operations and Finance at Whiteland Real Estate in Winnipeg. She leads budgeting, cash flow analysis, financing and acquisition analysis for a real estate investment and development company.

What Neha does for WealthScale

Neha reviews WealthScale's investing pages: broker, robo-advisor and crypto platform reviews, platform comparisons, best-of investing lists, and ETF and investing guides. She checks fees, account features, investment terms and worked examples against providers' own pages and regulator sources, and flags anything unclear or out of date. Tax rules, contribution limits and government benefits are reviewed by Sharad Gondaliya, CPA.

How Neha's review works on a page

Her scope. Neha reviews WealthScale's investing and account pages: platforms, brokers, robo-advisors, TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, RESP and bank accounts. On an account page, she checks the account features and provider terms. The tax rules on that page, such as contribution limits and withdrawal tax, are checked by Sharad.

What she checks. Her checks fall into five groups, each tested against providers' own pages and regulator sources.

What Neha checksExamples on a page
FeesCommissions, currency conversion fees, account fees and transfer-out fees
Account featuresWhich registered accounts a provider offers, USD accounts and account minimums
Investment termsHow a managed portfolio or robo-advisor charges, and how investing terms are defined
Worked examplesThe arithmetic in cost comparisons and hypothetical examples
Regulator factsCIRO membership and how CIPF and CDIC protection are described

Who has the final say. Tax rules, government benefits and final approval are Sharad's. Neha's review adds a second, investing-trained check; it does not replace his.

The review line. Every page in her scope shows a review line next to her name in the byline. Before she signs a page off, the line reads "Review of this page scheduled". Her review line shows a date on a page only after she signs that page off. If you see no date, the financial review of that page is not finished yet.

What her review is not. A review is an accuracy check. It is not an endorsement of any provider and not personal investment advice. Our editorial standards set out the full review process.
