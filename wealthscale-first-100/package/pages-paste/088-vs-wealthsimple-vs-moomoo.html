1. Wealthsimple vs moomoo: Quick Verdict

Start with the account you need. If you want an FHSA for a first home, an RESP for a child, a RRIF in retirement, a LIRA from an old pension, or a joint or corporate account, moomoo does not offer or list it, and Wealthsimple does. That alone can settle the choice before price comes into it.

Then look at how you buy. Wealthsimple lets you invest exact dollar amounts in fractional shares, set recurring buys and reinvest dividends automatically, all at $0 commission. moomoo buys whole shares only, charges at least CA$1.49 or US$1.99 an order, and leaves dividends in cash. For someone investing $100 or $500 at a time, those differences matter more than any headline rate.

Where moomoo has an edge. Its TFSA and RRSP hold US dollars with no fee stated, and its CAD margin starts at 3.85%. Those earn moomoo the better sub-scores on US investing and on cash and margin. It also charges a 0% currency conversion fee, but its exchange rate includes an undisclosed spread, so our test scores that conversion as not published. These edges are not enough to outweigh its account list, its per-share pricing and its missing features.

Wealthsimple. $0 commission on listed Canadian and US stocks and ETFs, on every tier. Pass-through exchange, ECN, TAF or ORF fees may apply to some US-listed orders. The main cost left is the 1.5% conversion fee on US trades from a Canadian-dollar account (section 3).

moomoo: a commission plus a platform fee. On Canadian stocks and ETFs moomoo charges CA$0.0049 a share in commission and CA$0.01 a share as a platform fee, CA$0.0149 a share combined, with a minimum of CA$1.49 an order. 3. Currency Conversion and US Dollars

moomoo: 0% fee, unpublished spread. moomoo charges a 0% currency exchange fee, which it describes as waived until further notice. Conversions use its Preferred Rate, which includes a spread of a size moomoo does not disclose. So the conversion is not free: you pay through the rate, and you cannot work out how much in advance. That is why moomoo sits in the "not published" group of our lowest currency conversion fees list, and why our scoring counts its conversions at the 1.5% median of brokers that do publish.

Wealthsimple: a published fee. A US trade from a Canadian-dollar account is converted at 1.5% on top of Wealthsimple's corporate exchange rate, on every tier. A USD account costs $10 plus tax a month on Core after a 30-day trial, and is included for Premium and Generation; inside it, each conversion costs 1.5% under $10,000, 1.0% from $10,000, 0.5% from $25,000 and 0% from $100,000, and US trades carry no conversion fee.

6. Fractional Shares, DRIP and Platforms

Fractional shares change how far small deposits go. A year of $100 deposits. This example is hypothetical. An investor adds $100 a month and buys a Canadian-listed ETF that stays at $40 a unit all year. At Wealthsimple each $100 buys 2.5 units at $0, so after 12 months they own 30 units ($1,200 ÷ $40). At moomoo they can buy only whole units after the CA$1.49 minimum, alternating between 2 and 3 units a month. After 12 months they own 29 units ($1,160 invested), have paid $17.88 in commissions (12 x $1.49) and hold $22.12 in uninvested cash ($1,200 - $1,160 - $17.88).

Moomoo or Wealthsimple: FAQ

Is Wealthsimple or moomoo better?

Wealthsimple scores higher: 4.0 / 5 and third of 13 in our ranking, against 3.3 / 5 and eighth for moomoo, as of October 22, 2026. Wealthsimple offers far more account types, fractional shares, DRIP and $0 commissions. moomoo suits active traders who want US dollars in a TFSA or RRSP with no fee stated and margin from 3.85%.

Does moomoo offer an FHSA or RESP?

No. moomoo Canada states that the FHSA, RESP, joint and corporate accounts are not available, and its account list does not include the RRIF, LIRA or LIF. It offers cash, margin, TFSA, RRSP and spousal RRSP accounts. Wealthsimple offers all of those plus the FHSA, RESP, RRIF, LIRA, LIF, joint and corporate accounts.

Does moomoo have fractional shares?

No. moomoo states that it does not support fractional shares, so every order is for whole shares and pays at least CA$1.49 or US$1.99. Wealthsimple offers fractional shares from $1 on thousands of Canadian and US stocks and ETFs at $0 commission, which lets a small deposit be invested in full.

Does moomoo offer DRIP?

No. moomoo states that DRIP is not available, though it offers auto-invest for regular purchases. Dividends stay in cash until you buy with them, at the usual commission. Wealthsimple lets you switch dividend reinvestment on, so dividends buy more units, including fractions, automatically.

Is moomoo cheaper than Wealthsimple?

Usually not. Wealthsimple charges $0 a trade; moomoo charges at least CA$1.49 or US$1.99 an order. On our scoring profiles, which count moomoo's spread at the 1.5% median, moomoo costs $149.64 a year for an active trader and $17.88 for a buy-and-hold investor, against $90.00 and $0.00 at Wealthsimple. moomoo can be cheaper for an active US trader who converts often, depending on its undisclosed spread.

Does moomoo charge a currency conversion fee?

moomoo charges a 0% conversion fee, which it says is waived until further notice, but its exchange rate includes a spread of undisclosed size, so conversion is not free. Wealthsimple charges 1.5% on US trades from a Canadian-dollar account, and 1.5%, 1.0%, 0.5% or 0% by size inside its USD account.

Can I hold US dollars in a moomoo TFSA?

Yes. moomoo says TFSA and RRSP holders can hold both Canadian and US dollars, with no fee stated. At Wealthsimple, a USD account can be added to a TFSA, FHSA, RRSP, spousal RRSP, LIRA, RESP or non-registered account, but not to a RRIF, spousal RRIF or LIF; it costs $10 plus tax a month on Core and is included for Premium and Generation.

Which has lower margin rates, moomoo or Wealthsimple?

moomoo's quoted rates start lower: CAD from 3.85% and USD from 6.4%, as of November 3, 2025. Wealthsimple charges 4.95% CAD on Core, 4.45% on Premium and 3.95% on Generation. On $20,000 for a year, that is $770 at moomoo's "from" rate against $990 at Wealthsimple Core.

Which is better for options trading?

Wealthsimple ranks higher, at 4.3 / 5 and second of 13, against 3.7 / 5 and fifth for moomoo. Wealthsimple charges $0 a contract on equity and ETF options; moomoo charges US$0.65 with a US$1 minimum. moomoo charges nothing on exercise or assignment, while Wealthsimple charges US$45 for an early exercise.

Is moomoo safe in Canada?

Moomoo Financial Canada Inc. states that it is regulated by CIRO and is on CIPF's member list, so eligible cash and securities are protected up to $1,000,000 per category if the firm fails. Wealthsimple Investments Inc. has the same protection. Both score 4.0 on our safety factor. CIPF does not cover market losses.

How much does it cost to transfer out of moomoo?

moomoo charges $75 for a full transfer or $50 for a partial transfer, per currency, plus sales tax, so a full transfer of an account holding both currencies costs $75 plus US$75, plus tax. Wealthsimple covers the other firm's transfer fee up to $150 per account on transfers of $25,000 or more that stay funded for 90 days.

Which is better for beginners, Wealthsimple or moomoo?

Wealthsimple. It ranks first of 13 in our beginners ranking at 4.5 / 5; moomoo ranks tenth at 3.3 / 5. Wealthsimple offers fractional shares from $1, automatic recurring buys, DRIP, no account minimum and $0 commissions. moomoo has no minimum deposit, but no fractional shares or DRIP, and charges per share.
