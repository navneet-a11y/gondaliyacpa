1. TD Direct Investing Review: Our Verdict and Score

The short version. TD Direct Investing names more trading platforms than any other broker we rate, publishes a low currency spread and has a strong safety score. It also has $9.99 standard commissions, a short list of account types on its account page and no published margin or cash rates. That combination scores 2.3 / 5, last of the 13 brokers on our best online brokers list, behind RBC Direct Investing (2.6) and Scotia iTRADE (2.4).

Why cost drags the score. Cost carries 35% of the score. On our active profile (two Canadian and one US trade a month, $6,000 converted a year) TD costs $435.24: 36 trades at $9.99 is $359.64 and $6,000 at 1.26% is $75.60. On buy-and-hold (one Canadian trade a month) it costs $119.88. The average, $277.56, gives a cost sub-score of 1.53 of 5. Seven of the brokers we rate charge $0 commission on every trade in both patterns.

Where TD earns points. TD is one of only two brokers with a perfect 5.0 on platform features, with Interactive Brokers Canada. It offers partial shares, DRIP enrolment, options, two advanced tools (Advanced Dashboard and TD Active Trader) and a mobile app. Its safety sub-score of 4.5 reflects CIPF membership and bank ownership.

Where it loses points. TD's account-types page lists only cash, FHSA, TFSA, RRSP and margin accounts for Direct Investing, so it scores 2.0 on accounts. Spousal RRSP, RESP, RRIF, LIRA, LIF, joint and corporate accounts were not listed on the page we checked, and we count only what is published. Margin and cash rates are not published either, which leaves 0.5 of 5 on cash and margin.

The bottom line. The score rates TD Direct Investing. If your trading fits inside TD Easy Trade's 100 $0 trades a year, your real costs at TD are far lower than the score suggests, and section 3 covers that app in detail. If you trade often, convert currency or want a broad account lineup at one $0 broker, the brokers ranked above TD will usually cost less. 3. TD Easy Trade: 100 $0 Trades a Year, Then $9.99

What TD Easy Trade is. TD Easy Trade is TD's app-based investing service, part of TD Direct Investing. It runs on phones and tablets only. It offers cash, TFSA, RRSP and FHSA accounts, has no maintenance fees and lets you start with $1, with dollar-amount orders from $1.

The 100 $0 trades. Each client gets the first 100 stock and ETF trades of the calendar year at $0, across Canadian and US markets. After that, trades cost $9.99, or US$9.99 for US trades. The count resets each January. 100 trades a year is just over 8 a month, so a monthly investor buying two or three ETFs stays well inside it. An order for less than one share costs $1.99 (US$1.99 in US dollars), so whole-share orders are the cheaper way to use the allotment.

US dollars in Easy Trade. Easy Trade's TFSA, RRSP and cash accounts each have a US dollar side, and TD says there are no fees for US dollar accounts. That lets you keep US dividends and sale proceeds in US dollars instead of converting them back. The FHSA is Canadian dollars only. TD does not publish a separate conversion table for Easy Trade; its published tiers are for TD Direct Investing.

What Easy Trade does not do. Options pricing is not stated for Easy Trade, and margin accounts are not among its account types. Spousal RRSP, RESP and RRIF accounts are not among the app's account types. If you need those, they belong in TD Direct Investing or at another broker, and our best investing apps page ranks the app-first alternatives.

The transfer offer has ended. TD Easy Trade offered 3% cash back on assets transferred into registered accounts and 1% on assets transferred into non-registered accounts. That offer ended on October 23, 2026. Check TD's current offers and their terms before you transfer.

How Easy Trade compares. RBC's GoSmart gives 50 $0 trades a year in its TFSA, FHSA and RRSP. Wealthsimple and Questrade charge $0 on every stock and ETF trade with no annual cap. Easy Trade's 100 $0 trades, its $1 start and its US dollar sides with no account fee make it the low-cost way into TD for a regular investor. Conversions still carry TD's spread, and the 101st trade of the year costs $9.99.

9. Is TD Direct Investing Safe? CIRO and CIPF

Who holds your account. The TD pages we checked did not name the dealer behind TD Direct Investing. TD Waterhouse Canada Inc. is on the Canadian Investor Protection Fund's member list, and every CIPF member firm is a member of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO), which oversees all investment dealers in Canada.

What CIPF covers. If a member firm becomes insolvent and client property is missing, CIPF covers the shortfall up to set limits per group of accounts. It does not cover losses from falling markets, unsuitable advice or a company you invested in going bankrupt. Crypto assets are excluded.

Our safety score. TD scores 4.5 of 5 for safety. Our rubric gives 3.5 when CIPF membership is confirmed and CIRO membership follows from it, and adds 1 for bank ownership. Brokers that state both memberships directly and are bank-owned, such as RBC Direct Investing, score 5.0.

CIPF and CDIC are different. Eligible bank deposits at a member of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation are covered up to $100,000 per insured category. Cash and securities held at an investment dealer fall under CIPF instead, with the limits shown above. Holding both a bank account and a brokerage account with one banking group does not make one protection replace the other.

Your part. Use a strong, unique password, turn on any two-step verification TD offers, and read each trade confirmation. No protection fund covers money you send to a fraudster yourself, so treat unexpected calls or messages about your account with caution.

10. Opening a TD Account and Transferring In or Out

Opening. You can start TD Easy Trade with $1. A minimum for TD Direct Investing was not stated on the pages we checked. A TFSA requires Canadian residency, a valid social insurance number and age 18 or older (19 where that is the age of majority). The $25 quarterly fee is waived during your first 6 months, which gives a new account time to reach $15,000 or set up a monthly contribution.

Transferring out. TD charges $150 to transfer an account out, full or partial, for both TD Direct Investing and TD Easy Trade. If TD charges the fee on each account you move, two accounts cost $300. Several receiving brokers cover part or all of that fee; the calculator below shows which brokers cover it and how much is left for you to pay.

Transferring in. TD did not state a transfer-fee rebate for accounts moving to it. TD Easy Trade's offer of 3% cash back on registered transfers and 1% on non-registered transfers ended on October 23, 2026, so a transfer to TD may leave you paying your old broker's fee yourself. RBC Direct Investing, by comparison, reimburses up to $200 on transfers of $15,000 or more.

Keep registered transfers direct. Ask the receiving institution to pull your TFSA, RRSP or FHSA directly. A direct TFSA transfer does not affect your contribution room, while a withdrawal and redeposit counts as a new contribution. A withdrawal from an RRSP is taxable and subject to withholding of 10%, 20% or 30% outside Quebec. Our guide to transferring an investment account covers the forms and timing.

Customer support. Support hours and channels were not part of the figures we checked, so we do not score them. DRIP enrolment at TD is done by phone, so you will need to call at least once if you want dividends reinvested.

10 Strategies to Keep TD Direct Investing Costs Low

1. Use Easy Trade for regular buying

If you place fewer than 100 stock and ETF trades a year, TD Easy Trade charges $0 for them. At standard Direct Investing prices, 36 trades would cost $359.64. Hold the accounts Easy Trade offers there, and use Direct Investing only for what Easy Trade cannot do.

2. Check the $0 ETF list first

TD Direct Investing charges $0 on select ETFs bought digitally. If a fund you want is on the list, a monthly purchase costs $0 instead of $119.88 a year. Confirm the $0 price before you submit the order.

3. Waive the quarterly fee

A $100 monthly pre-authorized contribution waives the $25 quarterly fee in Direct Investing, saving $100 a year. So do household assets above $15,000, 3 commission trades in the prior quarter, an RDSP, or an FHSA-only account.

4. Cross the $25,000 tier in one conversion

Converting $24,999 costs $314.99 at 1.26%; converting $25,000 costs $195.00 at 0.78%. If you need about that much in US dollars, one conversion at the tier saves $119.99 over doing it just below.

5. Keep US dollars in Easy Trade accounts

Easy Trade's TFSA, RRSP and cash accounts have US dollar sides with no account fee. Keeping US dividends and sale proceeds in US dollars avoids converting back and forth at the spread each time.

6. Hold US dividend payers in an RRSP

US dividends paid into an RRSP are exempt from US withholding under the treaty once the account is documented. In a TFSA, 15% is withheld and cannot be recovered, so the RRSP is usually the better home for US dividend payers.

7. Use partial shares sparingly

Each partial-share trade costs $1.99. On a $50 order that is about 4.0%. Buying whole shares in Easy Trade, inside the 100 $0 trades, avoids the charge altogether.

8. Do not chase Active Trader pricing

The $7.00 price needs 150 or more trades a quarter. Trading more to reach it costs more in total than it saves for almost any long-term investor. If you trade that much, compare a $0 broker first.

9. Ask for the margin rate before borrowing

TD does not publish its margin rates. Get the current rate in writing before you borrow, and compare it with the published rates on our margin page, which run from 3.675% to 8.70% under $100,000.

10. Move registered accounts directly

A direct transfer keeps TFSA room intact and avoids RRSP withholding of 10%, 20% or 30% outside Quebec. Leaving TD costs $150, so check whether the receiving broker covers it before you move.

TD Direct Investing Review FAQ

Is TD Direct Investing good?

It depends on how you invest. TD Direct Investing scores 2.3 / 5 overall, last of the 13 brokers we rate, mainly because of its $9.99 standard commission. It scores full marks on platform features and 4.5 on safety, and its 1.26% spread under $25,000 is low. TD Easy Trade, with 100 $0 trades a year, is far cheaper for small, regular investors.

What are TD Direct Investing's fees?

TD Direct Investing fees are $9.99 per stock or ETF trade ($7.00 for Active Traders with 150 or more trades a quarter), $0 on select ETFs bought digitally, $1.99 per partial-share trade, and $9.99 plus $1.25 per contract for options. Households under $15,000 pay $25 a quarter unless a waiver applies, and a transfer out costs $150.

How many $0 trades does TD Easy Trade include?

Each TD Easy Trade client gets the first 100 stock and ETF trades of the calendar year at $0, in Canadian or US markets. After that, trades cost $9.99, or US$9.99 for US trades. Orders for less than one share cost $1.99. Currency conversions carry TD's spread, and a transfer out costs $150.

How do I avoid TD Direct Investing's quarterly fee?

The $25 quarterly fee applies only when household assets are under $15,000. It is waived if you contribute $100 or more a month by pre-authorized contribution, placed 3 or more commission trades in the previous quarter, hold an RDSP, are in your first 6 months, or hold only an FHSA. TD Easy Trade has no maintenance fee.

What does TD charge to convert Canadian dollars to US dollars?

TD's published spread for Direct Investing is 1.26% under $25,000, 0.78% from $25,000, 0.46% from $100,000, 0.27% from $500,000 and 0.19% from $1,000,000. On $10,000 that is $126.00, the second-lowest published cost of the brokers we rate, after Interactive Brokers Canada. TD does not publish a separate Easy Trade table.

Can I hold US dollars in a TD TFSA or RRSP?

In TD Easy Trade, yes. Its TFSA, RRSP and cash accounts have US dollar sides, and TD says there are no fees for US dollar accounts. The Easy Trade FHSA is Canadian dollars only. TD does not state US dollar pricing for registered accounts in TD Direct Investing, so ask TD before you open one there.

What account types does TD Direct Investing offer?

TD's account-types page lists cash, FHSA, TFSA, RRSP, margin and Active Trader margin accounts for TD Direct Investing. Spousal RRSP, RESP, RRIF, LIRA, LIF, joint and corporate accounts were not listed on the page we checked on October 22, 2026, so ask TD if you need one. TD Easy Trade offers cash, TFSA, RRSP and FHSA accounts.

Does TD Direct Investing offer fractional shares?

Yes. TD Direct Investing and TD Easy Trade both offer partial shares at $1.99 per partial-share trade, and Easy Trade accepts dollar-amount orders from $1. On small orders the $1.99 is a large share of the purchase: about 4.0% of a $50 order.

What does TD charge for options trades?

TD charges $9.99 plus $1.25 per contract, or $7.00 plus $1.25 for Active Traders. A 2-contract trade costs $12.49, and four such trades a month cost $599.52 a year. That ranks TD 11th of 13 for options at 1.9 / 5. Options pricing is not stated for TD Easy Trade.

Is TD Direct Investing safe?

TD Waterhouse Canada Inc. is on the Canadian Investor Protection Fund's member list, and CIPF members are regulated by CIRO. If a member firm fails, CIPF covers missing property up to $1,000,000 for general accounts, $1,000,000 for retirement accounts and $1,000,000 for RESPs. It does not cover market losses.

How much does it cost to transfer out of TD Direct Investing?

TD charges $150 to transfer an account out, full or partial, for both TD Direct Investing and TD Easy Trade. Some receiving brokers cover the fee: RBC Direct Investing reimburses up to $200 on transfers of $15,000 or more, and Questrade rebates up to $150 per account.

Is there a TD Easy Trade transfer offer?

TD Easy Trade offered 3% cash back on assets transferred into registered accounts and 1% on assets transferred into non-registered accounts. That offer ended on October 23, 2026. Check TD's current offers and their terms before you transfer.

Is TD Automated Investing still available?

We could not confirm it. TD's investing hub lists Direct Investing, TD Easy Trade, TD Wealth and Invest with a Personal Banker, but not TD Automated Investing, and its product pages returned errors when we checked on October 22, 2026. We do not rate it. Our robo-advisor ranking lists four services we can verify.

We score every broker on cost (35%), accounts (15%), US investing (15%), cash and margin (10%), platform (15%) and safety (10%), using published prices only.
