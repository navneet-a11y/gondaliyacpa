How Sharad checks a page

Primary sources first. Each tax rule, contribution limit and government benefit figure is checked against primary sources: Canada Revenue Agency publications, Service Canada, provincial governments and, for US rules that affect Canadians, the IRS.

Every figure dated. Rates and fees are shown with the date they were checked, written as "as of October 22, 2026". Interest rates are re-checked weekly, fees monthly, and contribution limits each January and whenever a change is announced.

Arithmetic shown. Worked examples are labelled hypothetical, and every step of the arithmetic is on the page, so you can check each number and redo it with your own figures.

Final approval. Pages in Neha's scope go to her for a check of fees, account features and investment terms. A page goes live only after Sharad approves it. The full process is set out in our editorial standards.

What is the difference between a Canadian CPA and a US CPA?

In Canada, CPA stands for Chartered Professional Accountant, a designation granted by a provincial body such as CPA Ontario. In the United States, CPA stands for Certified Public Accountant, a licence issued by each state's board of accountancy. Sharad holds both: CPA Ontario membership and licences in Washington and Montana.
