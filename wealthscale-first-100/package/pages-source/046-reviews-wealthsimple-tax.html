7. NETFILE, Auto-Fill and Filing With Wealthsimple Tax

NETFILE certified. Wealthsimple Tax is certified by the Canada Revenue Agency and Revenu Quebec for the 2023, 2024 and 2025 tax years. Certification means the software can send your return directly to the CRA (and to Revenu Quebec for a Quebec return) instead of you mailing paper. All seven products in our ranking are certified for the 2025 tax year.

Auto-fill on every plan. Every Wealthsimple Tax plan can import the tax slips that the CRA and Revenu Quebec have on file for you. That saves typing and cuts the risk of missing a slip. Slips that arrive late, or that the issuer files late, may not appear, so compare the imported list with the paper and PDF slips you received before you submit.

Why filing matters even with little income. The CRA works out several benefits from your return. The Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, which replaced the GST/HST credit from the July 3, 2026 payment, pays up to $679 a year to a single person for July 2026 to June 2027. Filing a return, even on Basic for $0, is how the CRA assesses you for those payments. Our GST/HST credit and payment dates guide and CRA benefit payment dates page list the dates.

Deductions to claim before you file. RRSP contributions made by the deadline for the tax year reduce your taxable income; for the 2025 tax year, that deadline was March 2, 2026. FHSA contributions must be made by December 31 of the year, with no 60-day period. Our RRSP tax refund calculator and FHSA calculator show what each contribution is worth.

10. Wealthsimple Tax Maximum Refund Guarantee

The rule. Wealthsimple offers a maximum refund guarantee. If you re-file with a competitor before December 31, 2026, Wealthsimple refunds the competitor's price up to $50, or what you paid Wealthsimple, whichever is greater. The guarantee is built around getting a better result elsewhere, so read its full conditions before you rely on it.

What it means in practice. A Basic user who paid $0 and re-files with a $70 product would get $50 back, the capped competitor price. A Plus user who paid $40 and re-files with a $30 product would get $40, because their own payment is greater. The guarantee never pays more than the larger of $50 or what you paid Wealthsimple.

Does it matter. Certified tax software applies the same tax law, so a correctly entered return should produce the same result in any of them. Differences usually come from data entry, missed slips or missed claims, not from the software. The guarantee is a safety net, not a reason to expect a bigger refund.

Deadline. The re-filing window ends December 31, 2026 for returns filed under the current terms. If you think a claim was missed, the faster route is often to change your return with the CRA, which does not require switching software.

Do-it-yourself filers. If you are comfortable entering your own slips and claims, Basic gives you a full-featured return at a price you choose, including $0.

How Filing With Wealthsimple Tax Works

  1. Gather your documents. Collect every slip and receipt, and your last notice of assessment, before you start.
  2. Start a return. Begin on Basic. You can move to Plus or Pro later if you need more returns, faster support or an expert.
  3. Use auto-fill. Import the slips the CRA, and Revenu Quebec if you live in Quebec, have on file, then check them against your paper and PDF slips.
  4. Add what auto-fill cannot see. Enter self-employment income and expenses, the cost base of investments you sold, donations, medical expenses and other claims.

  6. Choose your price and file. Pick what you pay on Basic, including $0, or pay for Plus or Pro, then file by NETFILE.
  7. Keep your records. Save a copy of the return and your supporting documents in case the CRA asks for them later.

10 Strategies to File for Less With Wealthsimple Tax

1. Start on Basic

Basic covers every income type and deduction at a price you choose, including $0. Most single filers and couples never need more. Move to a paid plan only for a clear reason: more than 2 returns, faster support, audit protection or an expert.

2. Count your returns

Basic stops at 2 returns and Plus covers up to 8 for $40 plus tax. A household of three adults needs Plus. Group all the returns you prepare under one Plus purchase rather than buying it more than once.

3. Check your Wealthsimple tier first

If your assets at Wealthsimple, or your household's combined assets, reach $100,000, Plus is included. At $500,000, Pro is included outside Quebec. Check your tier before you pay for either plan.

4. Book Pro early

The Pro consultation was guaranteed only if booked before April 6, 2026. If you pay $80 for Pro, book the call early in the season so you are not left with a paid plan and no slot.

5. Use auto-fill, then check it

Auto-fill imports slips on every plan, which saves time and cuts errors. Compare the imported list with the slips you received, because a late or missing slip is the most common way a return goes wrong.

6. Claim every RRSP and FHSA receipt

RRSP and FHSA contributions are deductions, so a missed receipt costs you tax. For the 2025 tax year, the RRSP deadline was March 2, 2026, while FHSA contributions count only if made by December 31. See the RRSP contribution limit guide.

7. Track your cost base all year

The software reports what you enter. For stocks, ETFs and crypto, keep your adjusted cost base current through the year, including fees, so your capital gains are right and you do not overpay.

8. Confirm rental support before you start

T776 rental support is not confirmed in Wealthsimple's plan details. If you own a rental, check in the product before you begin, so you do not prepare most of a return and then have to switch.

9. File even with low income

The CRA assesses benefits such as the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit, up to $679 a year for a single person from July 2026 to June 2027, from your return. Filing on Basic for $0 costs nothing and keeps you assessed.

10. Know the refund guarantee terms

If you re-file with a competitor before December 31, 2026, Wealthsimple refunds the competitor's price up to $50, or what you paid, whichever is greater. Read the full conditions before you count on it.

Wealthsimple Tax Review FAQ

Is Wealthsimple Tax really free?

Basic can be. For the 2025 tax year, Wealthsimple Tax Basic is pay what you want, including $0, for up to 2 returns, with no limits on income types or deductions. You pay only if you choose to on Basic, or if you buy Plus at $40 plus tax or Pro at $80 plus tax for more returns, faster support, audit protection or an expert consultation.

How much does Wealthsimple Tax Plus cost?

Plus costs $40 plus sales tax for the 2025 tax year and covers up to 8 returns. It adds priority email support with replies in about 1 day, audit protection and spousal filing. Premium clients, with $100,000 or more in assets at Wealthsimple, and Generation clients get Plus included at no charge.

What does Wealthsimple Tax Pro include?

Pro costs $80 plus tax for the 2025 tax year and is included for Generation clients. It adds one 30-minute consultation with a tax expert, guaranteed if booked before April 6, 2026, year-round access to tax experts and audit protection. Pro is not available in Quebec or in French, and Wealthsimple's pages give two different counts for its return limit.

Is Wealthsimple Tax NETFILE certified?

Yes. Wealthsimple Tax is certified by the Canada Revenue Agency for NETFILE and by Revenu Quebec for the 2023, 2024 and 2025 tax years. That lets you send your return electronically from the software. All three plans can also import your tax slips from the CRA and Revenu Quebec through auto-fill.

Can I use Wealthsimple Tax in Quebec?

Yes, on Basic and Plus. Both plans file the federal and Quebec returns, work in French and can import slips from Revenu Quebec. Pro is not available in Quebec or in French, and its live consultation is not offered to Quebec residents, so a Quebec filer who wants expert help needs another product or a professional.

Does Wealthsimple Tax handle self-employment income?

Yes. Wealthsimple Tax has a section for form T2125, the statement of business or professional activities, and Basic covers all income types. A freelancer or sole proprietor can report business income and expenses without upgrading. You still need good records, because the software reports only the income and expenses you enter.

Does Wealthsimple Tax support rental income?

Not confirmed. Wealthsimple's published plan details do not confirm support for form T776, the statement of real estate rentals. If you earn rental income, check in the product before you start your return. Our tax software picker marks rental support as not confirmed for Wealthsimple and most other products.

How many returns can I file with Wealthsimple Tax?

Basic covers up to 2 returns and Plus up to 8, for the 2025 tax year. Wealthsimple's plan chart and FAQ give different figures for Pro, so we do not state one. A household filing 3 to 8 returns can buy one Plus plan for $40 plus tax, or get it included at the Premium tier.

What is the Wealthsimple Tax maximum refund guarantee?

If you re-file with a competitor before December 31, 2026, Wealthsimple refunds the competitor's price up to $50, or what you paid Wealthsimple, whichever is greater. A Basic user who paid $0 and re-files with a $70 product gets $50. A Plus user who paid $40 and re-files with a $30 product gets $40. Read the full conditions first.

Is Wealthsimple Tax better than TurboTax?

For people who prepare their own return, Wealthsimple scores higher, 4.8 / 5 against 4.5 / 5, because its pay-what-you-want Basic plan covers every income type while TurboTax Free covers simple returns only. TurboTax is stronger if you want expert help, with Expert Assist adding a final review and Expert Full Service preparing the return for you.

Is Wealthsimple Tax included with Premium or Generation?

Yes. Premium clients, with $100,000 or more in assets at Wealthsimple, get Plus included. Generation clients, with $500,000 or more, get Pro included, though Pro is not available in Quebec. Your tier is the higher of your net deposits or total portfolio value, and household members at the same address can combine assets.

Does Wealthsimple Tax import my tax slips?

Yes. Every plan, including Basic, can import the slips that the CRA and Revenu Quebec have on file for you. Check the imported list against the slips you received, because a slip filed late by its issuer may not appear, and add anything auto-fill cannot see, such as the cost base of investments you sold.
