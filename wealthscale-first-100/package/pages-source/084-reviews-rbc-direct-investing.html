1. RBC Direct Investing Review: Our Verdict and Score

The short version. RBC Direct Investing gets full marks for accounts and safety, charges no maintenance fee and reimburses up to $200 of transfer fees on transfers of $15,000 or more. It also charges $9.95 a trade, has one of the higher conversion spreads we track and does not publish margin or cash rates. That scores 2.6 / 5, 11th of 13 on our best online brokers list, ahead of Scotia iTRADE (2.4) and TD Direct Investing (2.3), and behind CIBC Investor's Edge (2.7).

Why cost drags the score. Cost carries 35% of the score. On our active profile (two Canadian and one US trade a month, $6,000 converted a year) RBC costs $454.20: 36 trades at $9.95 is $358.20 and $6,000 at 1.6% is $96.00. On buy-and-hold (one Canadian trade a month) it costs $119.40. The average, $286.80, gives a cost sub-score of 1.42 of 5, the lowest of the 13 brokers.

Where RBC earns points. RBC publishes all 10 account types we count, from the TFSA and FHSA to the RESP, RRIF, LIRA, LIF and corporate accounts, for a 5.0 on accounts. It states its CIRO and CIPF membership directly and is bank-owned, for a 5.0 on safety. Its platform sub-score is 4 of 5: DRIP, options, the Trading Dashboard and the RBC Mobile app count, while fractional shares are not mentioned.

Where it loses points. The US investing sub-score is 1.25 of 5: US trades cost $9.95, the spread under $25,000 is above 1.5%, and RBC does not state what a US dollar registered account costs. Cash and margin score 0.5, because neither rate is published on the pages we checked.

The bottom line. The score rates RBC Direct Investing's standard pricing. If your trades fit inside GoSmart's 50 $0 trades a year or RBC's $0 ETF list, your real costs are far lower than the score suggests. If you trade often, buy US stocks regularly or use margin, the $0 brokers ranked above RBC will usually cost less. 3. GoSmart: 50 $0 Trades a Year in the RBC Mobile App

What GoSmart is. GoSmart is RBC Direct Investing's mobile-first service, used through the RBC Mobile app. It gives 50 $0 stock and ETF trades a year, shared across GoSmart TFSA, FHSA and RRSP accounts. At the standard $9.95 price, those 50 trades would cost $497.50.

How far 50 trades go. 50 trades a year is just over 4 a month. A monthly investor buying one or two ETFs stays inside it, and 12 monthly purchases use only 12 of the 50. An investor buying four funds every month would use 48, close to the limit. Plan your purchases so you know where you are in the count before the year ends.

What GoSmart leaves out. GoSmart covers the TFSA, FHSA and RRSP only, so a non-registered, spousal RRSP, RESP or RRIF account sits outside it at standard prices. Options are not available in GoSmart. The price of a GoSmart trade after the first 50 of the year, and whether US trades count toward the 50, were not part of the figures we checked, so please confirm both with RBC before relying on GoSmart for US stocks.

The GoSmart offer. RBC pays $100 when you open a GoSmart account between August 4 and November 2, 2026 and deposit $2,000 by February 2, 2027. On a $2,000 deposit, $100 is a 5% return before any investment gain.

GoSmart vs TD Easy Trade. TD Easy Trade gives 100 $0 trades a year in cash, TFSA, RRSP and FHSA accounts, twice GoSmart's 50, and has US dollar sides in its TFSA, RRSP and cash accounts with no account fee. GoSmart sits inside a broker that offers a full account lineup and no maintenance fee, so your other accounts can live at RBC too. Our TD Direct Investing review covers Easy Trade in detail, and our best investing apps page ranks the app-first options.

Who GoSmart suits. A new investor making regular TFSA or FHSA contributions, or an RBC client saving for a first home in an FHSA, can invest for $0 in commissions within the 50-trade limit. Currency conversion, if you buy US funds, still carries RBC's spread.

9. Is RBC Direct Investing Safe? CIRO and CIPF

Who holds your account. Your account is held by RBC Direct Investing Inc., which states that it is a member of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO) and the Canadian Investor Protection Fund (CIPF). RBC Direct Investing Inc. is also on CIPF's member list. CIRO oversees all investment dealers in Canada.

What CIPF covers. If a member firm becomes insolvent and client property is missing, CIPF covers the shortfall up to set limits per group of accounts. It does not cover a fall in the value of your investments, unsuitable advice or the failure of a company you own shares in. Crypto assets are excluded.

Our safety score. RBC Direct Investing scores the maximum 5.0 for safety. Our rubric gives 4 when a broker states both CIRO and CIPF membership and adds 1 for ownership by a bank or financial cooperative group. TD Direct Investing scores 4.5, because its CIRO membership is inferred from CIPF membership rather than stated on the pages we checked.

CIPF and CDIC are different. Eligible bank deposits at a member of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation are covered up to $100,000 per insured category. Cash and securities at an investment dealer such as RBC Direct Investing fall under CIPF instead. Banking and investing with the same group does not make one protection replace the other.

Your part. Use a strong, unique password, turn on any two-step verification offered, and read each trade confirmation. No protection fund covers money you send to a fraudster yourself, so treat unexpected calls or messages about your account with caution.

10. Opening an Account, Transfers and RBC's Current Offers

Opening. RBC lists no minimum to open an account and states that there is no minimum balance. A TFSA requires Canadian residency, a valid social insurance number and age 18 or older (19 where that is the age of majority). Customer support hours and channels were not part of the figures we checked, so we do not score support.

Transferring in. RBC charges nothing to transfer an account in and reimburses up to $200 of the fee your old institution charges, on transfers of $15,000 or more. Royal Circle and Royal Distinction members get up to $500. If your old broker charges $150 on one account, the reimbursement covers it in full. If you move two accounts at $150 each and the $200 limit applies to your whole transfer, $100 is left for you; confirm with RBC how the limit applies before you move.

Transferring out. RBC charges $150 to transfer any account type out, except a donation of shares. RBC InvestEase charges $150 plus tax to transfer to a non-RBC account. Keep registered transfers direct: a direct TFSA transfer does not use contribution room, while a withdrawal and redeposit counts as a new contribution. 10 Strategies to Keep RBC Direct Investing Costs Low

1. Do regular buying in GoSmart

GoSmart's 50 $0 trades a year in a TFSA, FHSA or RRSP would cost $497.50 at the standard $9.95. Route your monthly registered purchases through GoSmart and keep standard trades for accounts GoSmart does not cover.

2. Use the $0 ETF list everywhere else

More than 50 selected ETFs trade at $0 in all RBC accounts, with no yearly cap. In a non-registered or spousal account, a fund on that list removes the $9.95 commission on every purchase.

3. Transfer $15,000 or more at once

RBC reimburses up to $200 of your old institution's transfer fee on transfers of $15,000 or more. Moving one large account rather than several small ones keeps you above the threshold.

4. Cross the $25,000 conversion tier

Converting $24,999 costs $399.98 at 1.6%; converting $25,000 costs $250.00 at 1.0%. When you need about that much in US dollars, one conversion at the tier saves $149.98.

5. Hold US dividend payers in an RRSP

US dividends paid into an RRSP are exempt from US withholding under the treaty once the account is documented. In a TFSA, 15% is withheld and cannot be recovered.

6. Ask about US dollar registered accounts

RBC's accounts can hold US dollars, but the registered pricing is not stated. If holding US dollars costs nothing extra, it lets dividends and sale proceeds stay in US dollars instead of converting at the spread each time.

7. Avoid small options trades

The $9.95 base fee is most of the cost on a 1- or 2-contract trade. If you trade options often, compare BMO's $0.90 a contract or Questrade's $0 on US equity options before you trade at RBC.

8. Compare InvestEase with a GoSmart portfolio

InvestEase costs 0.50% a year plus fund costs, or $100 plus up to $40 on $20,000. A self-built ETF portfolio in GoSmart costs $0 in commissions for up to 50 trades, if you are willing to manage it yourself.

9. Keep idle cash in a savings account

RBC does not publish what it pays on uninvested cash. Money you will not invest for months can earn a published rate in a high-interest savings account until you are ready to buy.

10. Respect the offer hold date

The Summer-Fall 2026 Cash Offer requires you to hold transferred assets until November 30, 2028, with payment in two parts. Moving the assets out early can cost the reward and adds a $150 transfer-out fee.

RBC Direct Investing Review FAQ

Is RBC Direct Investing good?

It depends on how you invest. RBC Direct Investing scores 2.6 / 5 overall, 11th of 13, mainly because of its $9.95 standard commission. It earns full marks for accounts and safety and charges no maintenance fee. If your trades fit inside GoSmart's 50 $0 trades a year or RBC's $0 ETF list, it can cost little in commissions.

What are RBC Direct Investing's fees?

RBC Direct Investing fees are $9.95 per online stock or ETF trade ($6.95 for Active Traders with 150 or more trades a quarter), $0 on more than 50 selected ETFs, and $9.95 plus $1.25 per contract for options. There is no maintenance fee, a transfer out costs $150, and the conversion spread is about 1.6% under $25,000.

How many $0 trades does RBC GoSmart include?

GoSmart gives 50 $0 stock and ETF trades a year, shared across GoSmart TFSA, FHSA and RRSP accounts, through the RBC Mobile app. Options are not available in GoSmart. The price after the first 50 trades and whether US trades count were not part of the figures we checked, so confirm both with RBC.

Does RBC Direct Investing charge a maintenance fee?

No. RBC states that it charges no quarterly or annual maintenance fee and has no minimum balance. Other costs still apply: $9.95 per standard trade, a conversion spread on US trades and $150 to transfer an account out.

What does RBC charge to convert Canadian dollars to US dollars?

RBC's spread is 230 basis points (about 1.6%) under $25,000, 145 basis points (1.0%) from $25,000, 85 basis points (0.6%) from $100,000, 50 basis points (0.4%) from $500,000, 25 basis points (0.2%) from $1,000,000 and no more than 10 basis points from $2,000,000. On $10,000 that is about $160.

Can I hold US dollars in an RBC TFSA or RRSP?

RBC says its investment accounts can hold Canadian and US dollar balances, but it does not state what a US dollar registered account costs. Ask RBC before you open one. US dividends paid into an RRSP are exempt from US withholding once the account is documented, while a TFSA has 15% withheld.

What does RBC InvestEase cost?

RBC InvestEase charges a flat 0.50% a year plus sales tax, billed monthly, with no lower tier for larger balances. Fund costs run up to 0.20% for Core portfolios and up to 0.25% for Responsible Investing portfolios. On $50,000 with Core portfolios, the total is up to $350 a year before sales tax.

How does RBC InvestEase compare with other robo-advisors?

RBC InvestEase scores 3.4 / 5, 4th of the 4 robo-advisors we rank. On $50,000 it costs 0.70%, against 0.37% at Questwealth Portfolios, 0.65% at Wealthsimple Managed Investing and 0.83% at CI Direct Investing. It scores well for its $100 minimum and phone or email advice, and lower for offering 4 of the 10 account types we count.

What does RBC charge for options trades?

RBC charges $9.95 plus $1.25 per contract, or $6.95 plus $1.25 for Active Traders. A 2-contract trade costs $12.45, and four such trades a month cost $597.60 a year, which ranks RBC 12th of 13 for options at 1.8 / 5. Options are not available in GoSmart.

Is RBC Direct Investing safe?

RBC Direct Investing Inc. states it is a member of CIRO and the Canadian Investor Protection Fund, and it is bank-owned, so it scores the maximum 5.0 on safety. If a member firm fails, CIPF covers missing property up to $1,000,000 for general accounts, $1,000,000 for retirement accounts and $1,000,000 for RESPs. It does not cover market losses.

Does RBC Direct Investing reimburse transfer fees?

Yes. RBC charges nothing to transfer in and reimburses up to $200 of your old institution's transfer fee on transfers of $15,000 or more, or up to $500 for Royal Circle and Royal Distinction members. Transferring an account out of RBC costs $150.

What is RBC Direct Investing's Summer-Fall 2026 offer?

Open an account through a promotion link between July 31 and November 2, 2026, and fund at least $2,500 by November 30, 2026. RBC pays up to 3% of net assets transferred into registered accounts and up to 1% into non-registered accounts, to a maximum of $15,000, if you hold until November 30, 2028. Payment comes in two parts.
