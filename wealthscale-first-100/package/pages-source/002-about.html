1. Who We Are: WealthScale Media Inc.

The publisher. WealthScale is published by WealthScale Media Inc., a federal corporation. Our office is at 55 Queen St E, Suite 1205, Toronto, ON M5C 1R6. WealthScale Media Inc. is responsible for every page, table and calculator on this site.

Who runs WealthScale. Sharad Gondaliya, CPA writes WealthScale's pages and gives final approval before any page goes live. Neha Gupta, CFA is our financial reviewer for investing and account pages. Both have full profiles with their credentials and links to verify them, so you can check who is behind what you read.

Who we write for. We write for Canadians who manage their own money. That includes people opening a first TFSA, choosing between a broker and a robo-advisor, moving savings to a better rate or cutting the currency fees they pay on US stocks. We assume no finance background and explain every term in plain language.

What the name means. The "Scale" in WealthScale means growth: helping savings scale up over time. It does not refer to a weighing scale. Our short version of the idea is three words: compare, choose, grow.

2. Our Mission: Compare Canadian Accounts on Real Costs

What "real costs" means. The headline price of an account rarely tells the whole story. A broker can advertise no commission on Canadian stocks and still charge a currency conversion fee on every US trade. A savings account can advertise a high rate that drops when a promotion ends. We put the costs that come out of your pocket side by side: commissions, currency conversion fees, account and administration fees, transfer-out fees, margin interest, and the interest you earn on cash.

Dated figures. Every rate and fee on WealthScale carries the date we checked it, written as "as of October 22, 2026". Interest rates are re-checked weekly, fees monthly, and contribution limits each January and whenever a change is announced. If a figure has not been published yet, we say so instead of guessing.

Calculators that do the arithmetic. Many pages include a calculator that runs your own numbers. Our investing, savings and tax calculators cover TFSA contribution room, RRSP tax savings, FHSA room, RESP grants, RRIF minimum withdrawals, compound growth, capital gains tax and brokerage currency conversion costs. The TFSA contribution room calculator is a good place to start.

Answers first. Each page opens with the answer, then gives the rules, exceptions, dates and worked examples behind it.

6. How We Rate and Rank Providers

Published scores. Our best-of lists, such as the best online brokers in Canada, rank providers by a published score out of 5, shown as "4.6 / 5". The order on the page follows the score. The factors and weights are explained in how we rate online brokers and robo-advisors.

The same criteria for every provider. Every provider on a list is measured against the same published criteria for that list.

No awards outside the score. We do not hand out stars, awards or "editor's picks" that sit outside the published score.

Costs worked out, not estimated. Where a list compares costs, we work out the yearly cost for sample investor profiles from each provider's published pricing and show the arithmetic.

What a score is not. A score is general information about how well a provider fits common needs. It is not a recommendation for you: the cheapest broker for one investor can be the wrong choice for another.

8. What WealthScale Does Not Do

No personal advice. WealthScale provides general information only. We do not know your income, goals, tax position or tolerance for risk, so nothing here is personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Canadian securities rules (National Instrument 31-103) allow general advice that is not tailored to the needs of the person receiving it, and we stay on that side of the line. For advice on your own situation, please speak with a qualified professional who can review your facts.

No stock, ETF or crypto picks. We do not tell you what to buy. We do not publish model portfolios, buy lists or price targets. When a review names a provider's own account types or managed portfolios, that is a description of the product, not a recommendation.

No paid rankings. No provider can pay to be included, scored higher or moved up a list (see section 6).

No invented evidence. We do not publish testimonials, made-up reviews, user counts or claims that we "tested" a product. Examples are labelled hypothetical, with the arithmetic shown.

No access to your money. WealthScale is not a bank, broker or dealer. We cannot open, see or change any account. Please never send us passwords, account numbers or your social insurance number. For a problem with an account, please contact the provider that holds it.
