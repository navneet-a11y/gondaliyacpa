Wealthsimple Fees: Every Fee by Product and Tier

WealthScale is independent and not affiliated with Wealthsimple.

Wealthsimple fees are low but not zero: stocks and ETFs trade at $0 commission and moving an account out costs $0, while US trades from a Canadian-dollar account pay a 1.5% currency fee on every tier. The USD account costs Core clients $10 plus tax a month, managed portfolios cost 0.5% a year on Core and 0.4% from $100,000, crypto market orders cost 0.5% plus a spread, and the credit card costs $20 a month unless waived. This guide puts every Wealthsimple fee in one master table by product and tier, shows what each costs in dollars, and explains how to avoid it. Figures were checked October 22, 2026.

What the tiers really change. Of the 41 rows above, only nine change with your tier: the USD account, the management fee, the staking share, the card fee, the domestic wire, the two margin rates and the two tax plans. 2. Trading Fees: What $0 Commission Covers

Stocks and ETFs. Wealthsimple's fee schedule lists a $0 commission for listed US and Canadian securities on every tier, and that includes fractional shares bought from $1. There is no per-trade minimum, no account fee and no inactivity fee. For an investor buying Canadian-listed ETFs once a month, the yearly trading cost is $0.00 in our buy-and-hold profile.

Options. Since October 2025, equity and ETF options cost $0 commission and $0 per contract. The one options charge is US$45 for an early exercise or a do-not-exercise instruction. Automatic exercise and assignment cost $0.

Phone trades. A trade placed by phone costs $45, unless you are an Active Trader (50 or more manual trades in a calendar month), in which case phone trades cost $0. The same order placed in the app or on the web costs $0.

Other assets. Physical gold costs 1% to trade, and redeeming it costs 11% for a tenth of an ounce or 2.25% for one ounce. Futures cost US$1 per contract plus a US$0.02 regulatory fee and exchange fees, and perpetual futures cost 0.12% of the notional value per trade. Event contracts cost US$0.02 per contract. Stock lending is not a fee, but Wealthsimple keeps half of the lending income and you keep 50%.

Pass-through fees. Some US-listed orders carry small exchange, ECN or regulatory fees that Wealthsimple passes on and shows on the trade confirmation. They sit outside the commission, which is why "commission-free" does not mean every trade costs exactly $0. Our Wealthsimple Trade review covers the full trading platform.

3. The Wealthsimple FX Fee: 1.5% and When It Applies

The basic rule. When you trade a US-listed security from a Canadian-dollar account, Wealthsimple converts your money at its corporate exchange rate and adds a 1.5% fee. The fee is identical on Core, Premium and Generation. It applies on the way in when you buy, on the way out when sale proceeds become Canadian dollars again, and to US dividends paid into a Canadian-dollar account, including those reinvested by DRIP.

Inside a USD account. A USD account changes two things. Trades in US-listed securities using US dollars you already hold carry no conversion fee. 4. The USD Account Fee: $10 a Month on Core

What it costs. On Core, the USD account costs $10 plus tax a month after a 30-day trial, or $120 a year before sales tax. There is no pro-rated refund if you cancel part way through a month. Premium and Generation clients pay nothing extra once they opt in. Wealthsimple lists this as its only paid add-on; there is no paid membership plan.

When it pays for itself on Core. Because conversions under $10,000 cost 1.5% inside or outside the USD account, small conversions save nothing. The saving starts at $10,000 (0.5% less, or $50.00 per $10,000 conversion) and jumps at $25,000 (1.0% less, or $250.00). Three conversions of $10,000 in a year save $150.00, which covers the $120 fee. One conversion of $25,000 saves $250.00, which covers it twice.

5. Managed Investing Fees by Tier

The management fee. Wealthsimple's managed portfolios cost 0.5% a year on Core, 0.4% on Premium and 0.4% on Generation, falling to 0.2% at $10,000,000 or more; Generation clients are asked to confirm their exact rate with the advisor team. The fee is charged on the managed balance, and the ETFs inside each portfolio charge their own fund costs on top: between 0.12% and 0.15% for Classic portfolios.

Automated Investing. This lower-cost option charges 0.25% a year and stops growing once the balance reaches $100,000, so the most you pay is $250 per account per year. On $10,000 it costs $25.00, on $50,000 $125.00, and on any balance of $100,000 or more $250.00. Specialty portfolios. Summit portfolios hold 20%, 25% or 30% in private markets, and the private portion carries a 1.00% management fee while the public portion pays the normal tiered fee. Advice at higher balances. A dedicated advisor team is available from $1,000,000. Wealthsimple's advisor-led Wealth Management service for households of $1,000,000 or more charges 0.8%, falling to 0.5% at $10,000,000, before fund costs.

Why a small percentage matters. A fee of 0.1% a year looks tiny, but it is charged every year on a growing balance. Our compound interest calculator shows how much more stays invested when yearly costs are lower.

Small costs that are easy to miss. Managed portfolios keep about 0.3% to 0.5% of the account in cash, and new deposits are invested within five business days, so a small slice of your money is not invested at any moment. Tax-loss harvesting, which can lower the tax you pay in non-registered accounts, is included only from Premium. 7. Wealthsimple Credit Card Fees and Interest

The monthly fee. Wealthsimple's Visa Infinite+ and Visa Infinite Privilege cards cost $20 a month, or $240 a year; Quebec cardholders are charged once a year. The fee is waived if you have $100,000 or more in individual assets at Wealthsimple or $4,000 or more a month in direct deposits. Both cards are offered in limited quantities, and Wealthsimple matches each applicant to one of them.

Does the cash back cover it? Both cards pay 2% cash back on all net purchases. At $240 a year, you need $12,000 of yearly spending, or $1,000 a month, before the cash back pays for the fee ($240 ÷ 2% = $12,000).

How to Audit Your Wealthsimple Fees

  1. List last year's conversions. From your account activity, add up every conversion between Canadian and US dollars, including US dividends paid into Canadian-dollar accounts, and multiply by 1.5%.

10 Strategies to Cut Wealthsimple Fees

1. Choose your US-dollar route once

There are three: a Canadian-dollar account at 1.5% each way, a USD account with tiered conversion rates, or Canadian-listed funds with no conversion. Picking one deliberately avoids paying 1.5% by accident on every buy, sale and dividend.

2. Move lump sums at $100,000

Inside a USD account, a single conversion of $100,000 or more costs 0%. If you are moving a large sum into US-dollar holdings anyway, one conversion at that size costs $0.00 against $1,500.00 from a Canadian-dollar account.

3. Know which accounts count toward your tier

Wealthsimple names the TFSA, RESP, RRSP, GRSP, LIRA, non-registered, Crypto, Save and chequing accounts as counting. It does not name the FHSA, RRIF or LIF in that list, so please confirm those before you plan an upgrade around them.

4. Protect your net deposits

Withdrawals, transfers out and conversions that carry an FX fee lower your net deposits. If you are near a tier line, a large withdrawal can push both measures below it and cost you the lower fees.

5. Match the managed option to your balance

At $50,000 on Core, managed portfolios cost $250.00 a year in fees and Automated Investing $125.00. At $200,000 on Premium, the gap widens to $800.00 against the $250.00 cap. Compare features as well as price.

6. Consolidate with the 1% match

Bringing scattered accounts together can clear the $25,000 minimum for the 1% match, if you are eligible. For fee coverage, move each account in one transfer of $25,000 or more rather than in smaller pieces. A $100,000 transfer earns $1,000.00, paid at $41.67 a month for 24 months.

7. Keep crypto orders few and priced

Fifty-two $50 market orders a year cost $65.00 in fees plus spreads. Thirteen $200 limit orders for the same $2,600 cost $13.00 with no spread.

8. Use e-Transfers before wires

Interac e-Transfers cost $0 and allow up to $50,000 a day for eligible clients, while a domestic wire costs $15 on Core and Premium. Save wires for amounts or recipients that need them.

9. Set up direct deposit

A direct deposit of $2,000 or more in 30 days lifts Core and Premium chequing by 0.5%. At $4,000 or more a month, it also waives the $240 card fee, which together can be worth more than a tier upgrade.

Wealthsimple Fees FAQ

What fees does Wealthsimple charge?

Listed stocks, ETFs and equity options trade at $0 commission. The main fees are 1.5% to convert currency on US trades from a Canadian-dollar account, $10 plus tax a month for the USD account on Core, 0.5% a year for managed portfolios on Core (0.4% from $100,000), 0.5% plus a spread on crypto market orders, and $20 a month for the credit card unless waived.

How much is the Wealthsimple FX fee?

Wealthsimple charges 1.5% on top of its corporate exchange rate when you trade US-listed securities from a Canadian-dollar account, on every tier. Inside a USD account, conversions cost 1.5% under $10,000, 1.0% from $10,000, 0.5% from $25,000 and 0% from $100,000 per transaction. Card purchases abroad carry 0% foreign exchange.

Is the Wealthsimple USD account worth $10 a month?

Only if you convert large amounts or trade US securities often. Conversions under $10,000 cost 1.5% inside or outside the USD account, so small conversions save nothing. One $25,000 conversion saves $250.00, which covers the $120 yearly fee. The account is included at no extra cost for Premium and Generation clients.

Does Wealthsimple charge a transfer fee?

Not to leave. Wealthsimple charges $0 to transfer an account to another institution, while most brokers we track charge $150 for a full transfer. Fractional shares cannot be transferred, so sell them or round them up first. A journaling request between your own Wealthsimple accounts costs $9.95.

Will Wealthsimple pay my old broker's transfer fee?

Yes, on transfers of $25,000 or more. Wealthsimple covers the transfer-out fee your old institution charges, up to $150 per account, as long as the account stays funded for 90 days. Its pages differ on whether this happens automatically or needs proof, so keep the statement that shows the fee.

What are Wealthsimple Premium fees?

Premium has no membership fee; you qualify with $100,000 in assets. Compared with Core, it cuts the managed fee from 0.5% to 0.4%, includes the USD account, includes Wealthsimple Tax Plus, waives the credit card fee and lowers the CAD margin rate from 4.95% to 4.45%. The FX fee on US trades stays at 1.5%.

How much does Wealthsimple Managed Investing cost?

The management fee is 0.5% a year on Core, 0.4% on Premium and 0.4% on Generation, falling to 0.2% at $10,000,000. Fund costs come on top, between 0.12% and 0.15% for Classic portfolios. On $50,000 at Core, that is $250.00 in fees plus at most $75.00 in fund costs. Automated Investing costs 0.25%, capped at $250 per account.

What does Wealthsimple charge for crypto?

Market orders cost 0.5% plus a spread, limit orders cost 0.5% with no spread, and orders under $100 add $1, under the fee schedule dated September 24, 2026. Wealthsimple keeps 30% of staking rewards on Core and Premium and 15% on Generation. Active Traders pay less, down to 0% at $15,000,000 of 30-day volume.

How do I avoid the Wealthsimple credit card fee?

The $20 monthly fee is waived with $100,000 or more in individual assets at Wealthsimple or $4,000 or more a month in direct deposits. Without a waiver, you need $12,000 of yearly spending before the 2% cash back covers the $240 fee. If you reach Premium only as a household, confirm the waiver applies to you.

Does Wealthsimple charge account or inactivity fees?

No. Opening, holding and closing an account cost $0, and Wealthsimple lists no inactivity or annual account fee. The only recurring charge on the investing side is the $10 monthly USD account fee for Core clients who choose it. Service charges, such as $20 paper statements, apply only when you request them.

Does Wealthsimple charge ATM fees?

Wealthsimple charges no ATM fee of its own on chequing withdrawals, and it reimburses eligible ATM operator fees worldwide, with no cap stated. The Visa Platinum Prepaid card has no annual fee and 0% foreign exchange. Instant withdrawals from an investing account to a debit card are different and cost 2.5%.

How much does Wealthsimple Tax cost?

For the 2025 tax year, Basic is pay what you want, including $0, for up to two returns. Plus costs $40 plus tax and is included for Premium and Generation clients. Pro costs $80 plus tax, adds a 30-minute expert consultation and is included for Generation clients. Pro is not offered in Quebec or in French.
