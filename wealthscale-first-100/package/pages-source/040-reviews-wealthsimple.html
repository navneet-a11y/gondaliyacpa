1. Wealthsimple Review: Our Verdict and Score

The short version. Wealthsimple earns 4.0 / 5 in our online broker ranking and places third of 13 Canadian brokers as of October 22, 2026. No other platform we score puts self-directed investing, managed portfolios, crypto, chequing, savings, a credit card and tax filing behind one login. For someone who buys and holds Canadian-listed ETFs, the only investing cost is the fund's own expense ratio.

Where it earns its score. Wealthsimple takes the full 5.0 for account types because it offers all 10 we count: TFSA, RRSP, spousal RRSP, FHSA, RESP, RRIF, LIRA or LIF, margin, joint and corporate. It scores 4.44 on cost, because $0 commissions leave only the currency fee in our active profile: $90 a year (1.5% x $6,000 converted). In our separate options ranking it takes the full 5.0 for options cost, since equity and ETF options carry $0 commission and $0 per contract.

Where it loses points. Cash and margin scores 2.0 of 5. US investing scores 3.0 because of the 1.5% conversion fee and the $10 monthly USD account fee on Core. The platform score is 4.0 because we do not count a separate advanced desktop trading platform.

Why it ranks third. Interactive Brokers Canada scores 4.4 / 5 on near-market currency conversion (0.20 basis points, minimum US$2) and the lowest margin rate in our ranking. Questrade also shows 4.0 / 5, but its unrounded score is 3.979 against Wealthsimple's 3.954, helped by US dollars in registered accounts at no extra cost. Wealthsimple wins outright for beginners: 4.5 / 5, first of 13, on its $1 fractional shares, simple app, dividend reinvestment and no stated account minimum.

Its other products score well too. Wealthsimple Managed Investing is first of four ranked robo-advisors at 4.2 / 5. Wealthsimple Crypto is first of nine authorized crypto platforms at 4.6 / 5. Wealthsimple Chequing is second of 20 chequing accounts at 4.4 / 5, behind EQ Bank. Wealthsimple Tax ranks first of seven certified tax products we score.

4. Self-Directed Investing: Wealthsimple Trade

Commissions and selection. Wealthsimple charges $0 commission on listed Canadian and US securities and offers more than 14,000 stocks and ETFs. You can buy fractional shares of thousands of Canadian and US stocks and ETFs from $1, in real time during market hours. Recurring investments can run daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and dividend reinvestment can be switched on for an account.

Options at $0 per contract. Since October 2025, equity and ETF options carry $0 commission and $0 per contract on every tier. In registered accounts such as the TFSA and RRSP you can buy calls and puts, sell cash-secured puts and write covered calls. Every other strategy needs a margin account. An early exercise or do-not-exercise instruction costs US$45.

Trading hours. US-listed stocks and ETFs trade 24 hours a day, five days a week, from Sunday 8 pm to Friday 8 pm Eastern time. That covers pre-market (4:00 am to 9:30 am), regular hours (9:30 am to 4:00 pm), post-market (4:00 pm to 8:00 pm) and an overnight session (8:00 pm to 4:00 am). Orders outside regular hours must be limit orders, fractional shares do not trade then, and Canadian securities are not part of 24-hour trading.

Margin and borrowing. Margin rates are 4.95% on Core (prime plus 0.5%), 4.45% on Premium (prime) and 3.95% on Generation (prime minus 0.5%) in Canadian dollars, using a CAD prime rate of 4.45% as of September 18, 2026. US-dollar margin costs 7.5%, 7% and 6.5%. Interest accrues daily and is charged monthly. Some clients get a restricted margin account with a $1,000 borrowing limit, and a TFSA can be linked to a margin account for buying power. Wealthsimple's Core rate ranks third in our lowest margin rates in Canada list.

Beyond stocks. Physical gold trades around the clock with a 1% trading fee, and redeeming physical gold costs 11% for a tenth of an ounce or 2.25% for one ounce. Futures cost US$1 per contract plus a US$0.02 regulatory fee and exchange fees, and perpetual futures cost 0.12% of the notional value per trade. Event contracts cost US$0.02 per contract. Private equity and private credit funds start at $10,000 and are open to every tier. If you lend shares through the stock lending program, you keep 50% of the income.

App, web and Active Trader. Everything runs in the Wealthsimple mobile app and the web app. Clients who place 50 or more manual trades in a calendar month become Active Traders, with one month of grace. They get phone trades at no charge (normally $45), phone pickup in under a minute, email replies within four hours and lower crypto fees. 5. Managed Investing and Automated Investing

The fee by tier. Wealthsimple's managed portfolios cost 0.5% a year on Core, 0.4% on Premium and 0.4% on Generation, falling to 0.2% at $10,000,000 or more. Generation clients are told to confirm their exact rate with the advisor team. Corporate managed accounts pay 0.5%, dropping to 0.4% at $100,000. The fund costs of the ETFs inside the portfolio come on top: Classic portfolios use ETFs with expense ratios between 0.12% and 0.15%.

The portfolios. The current lineup is Classic, Summit, Income, the Private Market Fund, Automated Investing and Advice. Classic holds eight to ten ETFs and rebalances automatically when you deposit, withdraw or change your goal, with dividends reinvested into underweight funds. Summit adds private markets at 20%, 25% or 30% for Balanced, Growth and Aggressive, with a 1.00% management fee on the private portion. It needs $10,000 of investable assets, $3,500 before the private portion is added, and a horizon of four years or more. A Socially Responsible portfolio with expense ratios between 0.21% and 0.23% is described in Wealthsimple's help centre.

Income portfolios. Wealthsimple offers three: Vanguard Income, Core Bond and a Money Market portfolio. The Money Market portfolio has no management fee; the other two pay the tiered fee. You can hold one Income portfolio per account.

Automated Investing. This option costs 0.25% a year and is capped once the balance reaches $100,000, so the most you pay is $250 per account per year. In non-registered accounts it uses direct indexing with tax-loss harvesting, and there is no currency fee inside the direct indexing sleeves (1.5% applies outside them).

What higher tiers add. Tax-loss harvesting comes with Premium and Generation, not Core. Asset location and custom portfolios are Generation-only. A dedicated advisor team is available from $1,000,000, and Wealthsimple's advisor-led Wealth Management service for households of $1,000,000 or more charges 0.8%, falling to 0.5% at $10,000,000, before fund costs.

How it ranks. Wealthsimple Managed Investing scores 4.2 / 5 and ranks first of four robo-advisors we can rank, on its portfolio range, $1 entry and advisor access. Its weak spot is cost: about 0.65% a year in total at $50,000 on Core (0.5% fee plus up to 0.15% in fund costs), against 0.37% for Questwealth Portfolios. 6. Wealthsimple Crypto

Fees. Under the fee schedule dated September 24, 2026, crypto market orders cost a flat 0.5% plus a spread on every tier, and limit orders carry no spread. Orders under $100 add a $1 charge, so a $50 market order costs $1.25 in fees ($50 x 0.5% = $0.25, plus $1), or 2.5% of the order, before the spread. Active Traders pay less: 0.35% from $10,000 of 30-day volume, 0.25% from $100,000, 0.15% from $500,000, 0.10% from $1,000,000, 0.05% from $10,000,000 and 0% from $15,000,000, with no spread.

Coins and staking. Wealthsimple lists more than 170 coins. You can stake ETH, SOL, ADA and DOT, with yields of up to 3.8% a year on Core and Premium and up to 5% on Generation, shown net of fees and set by each network. Wealthsimple keeps 30% of staking rewards on Core and Premium and 15% on Generation, and validator commissions are separate. A built-in decentralized exchange feature charges a 0.85% trading fee, with network fees currently covered.

Transfers and limits. You can send crypto in and out; the blockchain sets the network fee and Wealthsimple lists no transfer fee of its own. Crypto is held only in a non-registered crypto account, not in a TFSA, RRSP or FHSA. Under its regulatory terms, investors who do not qualify as eligible can buy up to $30,000 of net crypto cost in a 12-month period, eligible investors up to $100,000, and accredited investors have no limit, outside certain provinces.

Regulation and custody. Wealthsimple Investments Inc. has been a registered crypto trading platform since January 1, 2024, and is a CIRO investment dealer. Most crypto sits in offline cold storage with regulated custodians, held in trust, and each custodian partner carries $75 million or more of cold storage coverage. Hot wallets are covered through Coincover. Crypto assets are not protected by CIPF or CDIC, though cash in the crypto account is CIPF-covered.

How it ranks. Wealthsimple Crypto scores 4.6 / 5, first of nine platforms on the Canadian Securities Administrators' authorized list that we rank. NDAX charges less at 0.20% flat, but scores lower on coin selection and regulation. Read the Wealthsimple Crypto review or compare all nine in our best crypto platforms in Canada ranking.

7. Wealthsimple Chequing and Savings

Chequing rates. Wealthsimple Chequing pays 1.25% on Core, 1.75% on Premium and 2.25% on Generation, calculated daily and paid monthly. A direct deposit of $2,000 or more within a 30-day period adds 0.5% for Core (to 1.75%) and Premium (to 2.25%). Generation already earns 2.25% and gets no boost. On a $10,000 balance for a year, that is $125.00 on Core, $175.00 on Premium and $225.00 on Generation before tax.

Everyday costs. The account has a $0 monthly fee, no minimum balance and $0 Interac e-Transfers, with up to $50,000 a day for eligible clients. The Visa Platinum Prepaid card has no fee and 0% foreign exchange on purchases. Wealthsimple charges no ATM fee of its own and reimburses eligible ATM operator fees worldwide, with no cap stated. The card does not work in Costco Canada warehouses, which take only Mastercard.

Deposits, cheques and bills. You can deposit cash at more than 5,300 Canada Post locations and deposit cheques by photo, with funds in about six business days. A chequebook comes at no charge once your pay is direct deposited, and pay can arrive up to one day early. You can pay bills and order bank drafts in the app. Outgoing domestic wires cost $15 (waived for Generation), incoming wires cost nothing, and international SWIFT wires are available. Overdraft carries no fee, with interest from 3.95%. You can hold up to eight individual and eight joint chequing accounts. The institution number is 703 and the transit number is 00001.

Savings account. The savings account pays 2.5% to all clients, not as a promotion, with $0 fees, no minimum and no withdrawal limits or notice (rate data as of August 19, 2026). It is individual and non-registered only for now. It has no account or transit number, so you cannot direct deposit into it. On $20,000 for a year, 2.5% earns $500.00 before tax.

Kids and registered savings. The Kids and Teens account pays up to 2.25% with $0 fees, for up to 10 children per parent. Wealthsimple also offers a registered savings option inside the TFSA, RRSP and FHSA, protected by CDIC up to $100,000 per category rather than CIPF; its rate differs across Wealthsimple's own pages and is being confirmed.

How it ranks. Wealthsimple Chequing scores 4.4 / 5 and ranks second of 20 in our best chequing accounts ranking, behind EQ Bank at 4.6 / 5. It takes full marks for fees, transactions and ATM access and loses points on interest and because Wealthsimple is not a CDIC member in its own right. 8. USD Accounts and Currency Costs

The 1.5% rule. If you buy a US-listed stock or ETF from a Canadian-dollar account, Wealthsimple converts your money at its corporate exchange rate plus a 1.5% fee. The rate is the same for Core, Premium and Generation, and it applies again when US-dollar proceeds or dividends are converted back. For many investors this is the largest cost at Wealthsimple, and it is why it ranks third, not first, among the brokers with the lowest currency conversion fees.

How a USD account changes it. Inside a USD account, there is no conversion fee when you trade US-listed securities with US dollars you already hold. When you convert between Canadian and US dollars inside the USD account, the fee depends on the size of each transaction, not on your tier: 1.5% under $10,000, 1.0% from $10,000, 0.5% from $25,000 and 0% from $100,000.

10. Core vs Premium vs Generation: Is Wealthsimple Premium Worth It?

How the tiers work. Every client starts on Core from $1. Premium starts at $100,000 and Generation at $500,000. Wealthsimple uses the higher of your net deposits or your total portfolio value, so a market drop alone does not demote you; you move down only if both fall and stay below the threshold. Withdrawals, transfers out and conversions that carry an FX fee reduce net deposits. Upgrades are automatic and take at least one business day, and new deposits can take up to 72 hours to count.

Households. Two or more Wealthsimple clients who share a home address can form a household and combine assets toward Premium or Generation. No size limit or relationship rule is stated. Members choose what to share, and others can see shared balances but cannot move money. Please note that the credit card fee waiver is written as $100,000 or more in individual assets, so confirm it applies before relying on a household upgrade for the card.

10 Strategies to Cut Your Wealthsimple Costs

1. Convert in large blocks

Inside a USD account, a $25,000 conversion costs 0.5%, or $125.00, against $375.00 at 1.5% on US trades from a Canadian-dollar account. Converting $24,999.99 instead costs $250.00, so check the size of each conversion against the tier boundaries before you place it.

2. Hold Canadian-listed funds where they fit

Canadian-listed ETFs trade in Canadian dollars, so buying them carries no conversion fee at all. Many hold US or global stocks inside the fund. Compare their expense ratios with the 1.5% fee charged on each conversion, in and out, before you choose a US listing.

3. Pool assets as a household

Clients who share a home address can combine assets toward Premium. On $100,000 of managed assets, the drop from 0.5% to 0.4% saves $100 a year, and the USD account and Tax Plus come included.

4. Use limit orders for crypto

Crypto limit orders carry no spread, while market orders add one on top of the 0.5% fee. Keep each order at $100 or more: below that, a $1 charge applies, which is 2.5% of the order on a $50 purchase including the 0.5% fee.

5. Send your pay to chequing

A direct deposit of $2,000 or more in 30 days adds 0.5% to the Core and Premium chequing rates. On a $10,000 balance, Core interest rises from $125.00 to $175.00 a year.

7. Time transfers of $25,000 or more

Transfers of $25,000 or more get the old institution's fee covered, up to $150 per account. If the 1% transfer match applies to you, transferring $30,000 earns $300, paid as $12.50 a month for 24 months.

8. Avoid instant withdrawals and paper

Instant withdrawals to a debit card cost 2.5%, or $25.00 on $1,000. Standard transfers to your bank avoid that fee, and choosing electronic statements avoids the $20 paper statement charge.

9. Make the card fee pay for itself
