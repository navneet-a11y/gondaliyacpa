Baseline: the October 9, 2026 catalogue. Machine-readable copy: `facts-wealthsimple.json`.

**192 facts:** 144 verified, 14 changed since Oct 9, 17 conflict, 17 unknown.

Status key: **verified** = confirmed on the cited page today. **changed since Oct 9** = confirmed today but differs from, or is missing from, the Oct 9 catalogue. **conflict** = two Wealthsimple pages (or two parts of one page) disagree; both values shown, separated by `|`. **unknown** = could not be confirmed; do not publish.
