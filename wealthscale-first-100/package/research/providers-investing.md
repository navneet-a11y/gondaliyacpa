**Key takeaways**

- **Free trading:** Seven of 13 brokers charge $0 online stock and ETF commissions to everyone: Wealthsimple, Questrade, Webull, Qtrade, NBDB, BMO and Disnat. Others offer limited free trading:
  - TD Easy Trade gives the first 100 trades a year free.
  - RBC GoSmart gives 50 free trades a year.
  - CIBC charges $0 only for under-25s.
  - Scotia gives up to 100 free trades with a Scotiabank banking package.
- **Still charging per trade:** CIBC ($6.95), TD Direct Investing ($9.99), RBC full-suite ($9.95) and Scotia iTRADE ($9.99). IBKR and moomoo charge per share instead, and are cheaper for small share counts.
- **FX is now the main cost driver on US trades.** On a $10,000 conversion:
  - IBKR costs about US$2.
  - moomoo charges no fee, but its spread is undisclosed.
  - TD's spread is 1.26%.
  - Wealthsimple, Questrade and Webull charge 1.5%.
  - CIBC, RBC and BMO charge about 1.6%, and NBDB about 1.7%.
- **Avoiding FX:** Holding USD in registered accounts avoids repeat conversions. It is free at Questrade, TD Easy Trade and Wealthsimple Premium/Generation. Wealthsimple Core pays $10/month, and Qtrade charges US$15/quarter (free with Investor Plus).
- **BMO's September 2026 change:** The current pricing page confirms $0 stock and ETF commissions and $0.90 per options contract. **The September 2026 effective date was not stated on any BMO page we could open**, so the date is unverified.
