# OpenCorp Master Blog-Post Prompt

Saved 2026-10-26 from Navneet. This is the house prompt for writing service blog posts for Open Corporation for $35 (opencorporationfor35.ca).

## How to reuse for a new topic

Keep sections 1–13 as they are. For each new post, change only the parts tied to the topic:

- The `Service`, `BLOG TITLE`, `PRIMARY KEYWORDS` and `SECONDARY KEYWORDS` lines.
- In 1, the topic sentence ("for ... deciding ...") and the "Mandatory: one 2026 update" list.
- In 2, the framing for the 10 industry spotlights. The industries themselves stay the same.
- In 3, the topic-specific sources and the integrity-gate items.
- In 4, the H2s after "Who This Is For". Keep them as search questions in the same order: concept, how it works, comparisons, cost, DIY vs CPA, process, deliverables, risks, prep, industries, walkthrough, choosing a CPA, trust, PAA, FAQ, glossary, next steps, SEO pack, sources.
- In 5, the topic-specific table names and columns. Keep at least 16 tables.
- In 6, the example scenarios and metrics.
- In 11.1, the topic-lock key numbers.
- In 12.4, the example "Risk Warning:".
- In 12.5, the estimator or widget inputs and outputs.
- In 12.7, the lead-magnet anchor.

Posts written with this prompt live in `opencorp/posts/`.
